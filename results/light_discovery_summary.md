## Site quirks for the deep pass (where policy documents and BIs live)

- **Bharti Life** (bhartiaxa.com): each product page (`/savings-plans/<plan>`, `/ulip-plans/<plan>`, `/term-insurance/<plan>` …, all in `sitemap.xml`) has three anchors: *Download Brochure*, *Policy Bond* (policy document, often named `…part_b.pdf`) and *Customer Information Sheet*, all under `/sites/default/files/<yyyy-mm>/`. Every page also embeds a few stale 2023 brochures (Wealth Pro, SIGP, Flexi Term Pro), so don't treat a `.pdf` on a product page as that product's file. Grow Wealth Ultra's brochure prints Dream Shield Plus's UIN, so its policy bond was used. Monthly Advantage's brochure prints `130NO68V05` (letter O). No BIs online.
- **LIC** (licindia.in): category pages (`/web/guest/endowment-plans`, `…/whole-life-plans`, `…/money-back-plans`, `…/term-assurance-plans`, `…/pension-plan`, `…/unit-linked-plans`, `…/micro-insurance-plans`) are tables of plan name → plan page, plan no., UIN. Each plan page links *Sales brochure* (sometimes two, dated "From … To …" and "From … onwards"), *Policy Document* and *CIS* under `/documents/20121/…`. LIC prints the par flag before "Linked" ("A Par, Non-Linked, …").
- **SBI Life**: product pages link `/documents/d/sbil/product-brochure-prospectus-NN`-style URLs (no `.pdf` suffix). Policy documents are linked from the same pages. Smart Annuity Plus had no verifiable brochure, so its policy document was used.
- **Generali Central** (generalicentrallife.com, Next.js): product pages (from `sitemap.xml`) embed `/docs/<plan_slug>/product_brochure.pdf`, `/docs/<plan_slug>/policy_document.pdf`, `customer_information_sheet.pdf` and `policy_schedule.pdf` in page data. Brochures print the par flag in brackets before "Unit linked": "Non-Participating (without profits), Unit linked, …".
- **HDFC Life**: product pages (from `universal-sitemap.xml`) don't link the product brochure directly (only rider brochures). Current files are on `/brochures` (folders `…/brochure-documents-pdfs/<line>-live/`) and `/policy-documents` (folders `…/policy-documents-pdf/<line>-live/` and `…-withdrawn/`). `pdf-sitemap.xml` lists every PDF the site hosts, including old versions. 13 master-list products appear on `/policy-documents` only under *withdrawn* sections (noted per row). Several brochures on the site are older versions than the master-list UIN (see the versions table).
- **Tata AIA**: product pages from `sitemap.xml`; brochure and policy-document links under `/content/dam/tataaialifeinsurancecompanylimited/life-insurance-plans/<line>/<plan>/`. Some product pages only expose a policy bond. `active-and-withdrawn-product-status.html` lists every UIN version with launch and withdrawal dates (useful for status checks). POS variants have no pages.
- **Shriram Life**: documents are on per-product `…/life-insurance/<plan>-download-documents` pages (linked as *Downloads* from each product page). Links live in embedded Nuxt data on `cdn.shriramlife.com/slic-kalam/files/<yyyy-mm>/…` with `\u002F`-escaped slashes and `?VersionId=` suffixes. The same pages carry the policy document and a product presentation. Smart Choice Plan prints its description line only as an image (OCR).
- **Aditya Birla Sun Life (ABSLI)**: all documents under `lifeinsurance.adityabirlacapital.com/uploads/…`. The *brochures and specimen contracts* page (seed) lists brochures and policy contracts for every product; product pages are in `sitemap.xml`.
- **Acko Life**: `acko.com/life/download/` lists the brochure ("Product Brochure (Effective …)"), policy document, CIS and **benefit illustration** for both products, hosted on Acko's asset domain `acko-cms.ackoassets.com`. The *Download* button on `/life-insurance/` opens that page through JavaScript.
- **Ageas Federal**: product pages (`/savings-plans/…`, `/ulip-plans/…`, `/term-insurance-plans/…` in `sitemap.xml`) link files on Azure blob storage (`sacicwpprd01.blob.core.windows.net/$web/sccicwppr01/assets/…`). Two pages link a second account, `sacicwputd01`, whose name suggests a test (UAT) environment (Super Protect Plus, Young Star Plus); flagged per row.
- **Aviva**: `/sites/default/files/…` on product pages; `form-and-resource-help-desk` (seed) lists many brochures. Bharat Bal Vikas Yojana's sales literature is a scanned image PDF (OCR).
- **Axis Max Life**: product pages (from `main-sitemap.xml`) link *Policy Prospectus*, *Policy Contract* and *Policy Leaflet* under `/static-page/assets/homepage/<CODE>_Prospectus_<hash>.pdf`.
- **Bajaj Life**: `/content/dam/balic-web/pdf/<line>/<plan>-brochure.pdf`, with `-cis.pdf` and `-leaflet.pdf` next to it; `on-sale-plans.html` (seed) links every product page. Two master rows without a UIN are combination offerings (see flags).
- **Bandhan Life**: `/sites/default/files/asset/<yyyy-mm>/…` on product pages (`sitemap.xml`).
- **Canara HSBC**: product pages (`sitemap.xml`) link `/content/dam/chli/pdf…/<plan>/…/product-brochure.pdf`, with `policy-document.pdf` and `customer-information-sheet.pdf` in the same folder. Several brochures are image-heavy (UIN only in images, so OCR).
- **Edelweiss Life**: Liferay site; product pages link `/documents/d/guest/<slug>_productbrochure` (no `.pdf`) and `/documents/20121/<id>/…_ProductBrochure.pdf`, plus sample policy, sample rates table and CIS on the same page.
- **IndiaFirst Life**: brochures under `/content/dam/ifliwebsite/in/en/download-center/all-product-related-documents/brochures/individual/<plan>/…pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf`; `pdf-sitemap.xml` lists every PDF, including a `withdrawn-products/` folder.
- **IndusInd Nippon Life**: Liferay `/documents/34625/<id>/…` links on `all-plans/<line>/<plan>` pages. Some product pages link only a mortality-rate table or the online-sales copy on `onlinesales.indusindnipponlife.com`.
- **PNB MetLife**: `/content/dam/pnb-metlife/docs/product/Download_Brochure/…` on `insurance-plans/<line>/<plan>.html`; `TermsCondition/` holds policy documents and `leaflet/` one-pagers.
- **Pramerica Life**: uploads under `pramericalife.in/admin/assets/fileUpload/…` and `…/images/pages/…` linked from product pages (`sitemap-index.xml`).
- **Leaflets vs brochures (all sites):** ABSLI, SBI, Bandhan, PNB MetLife, HDFC and others publish a one-page *leaflet* next to the full sales brochure, often linked more prominently. The script ranks brochure > policy document > leaflet and labels leaflets `doc_type=leaflet`; only one leaflet is stored (Bajaj Smart Secure ROP, a combination offering with nothing else published).
- **Blocked sites:** ICICI, Kotak, Star Union Dai-ichi and Go Digit were not contacted (brief). Their 106 products are `manual`, with the page to visit, in `results/manual_checklist.csv` and `results/manual_checklist_blocked_sites.csv`.
