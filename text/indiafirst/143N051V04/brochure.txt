Plan at a Glance
1. What is the IndiaFirst Life Smart Pay Plan?
This a non-linked, participating, individual, money back savings life insurance plan which not only provides
shorter pay commitments but also gives some liquidity within premium paying period. This plan is designed
to ease the fulfilment of your financial goals through money back during the premium paying period itself,
continuation of your life cover benefit in case you miss to pay one premium, giving you an upside in the form
of bonus (if declared) at maturity while protecting your loved ones through a life cover in case of any
unfortunate event.
