How the plan works
The IndiaFirst Life Waiver of Premium Rider can be attached to your base policy (currently, IndiaFirst Maha Jeevan Plan) to
protect you from the adverse effects of unfortunate events like Death, Accidental Total Permanent Disability and Critical
Illnesses. You can choose from one of the following options to further protect your loved ones.
•   Waiver of Premium on Death
•   Waiver of Premium on Accidental Total Permanent Disability or (diagnosis of) Critical Illness
•   Waiver of Premium on Death or Accidental Total Permanent Disability or Critical Illness
Let us understand this with the help of an example. Kumar had bought the IndiaFirst Maha Jeevan Plan for his daughter Kiara’s
(3 year old) education for an annual premium of INR 20,000 and a term of 15 years. While he received the benefit of protection
and savings in one plan, he wanted to enhance it further. So, he attached the IndiaFirst Life Waiver of Premium Rider with the
plan option of Waiver of Premium on Death, Accidental Total Permanent Disability and Critical Illness. In case Kumar suffers
from Accidental disability during these 15 years, Kiara would still receive the maturity benefit of IndiaFirst Maha Jeevan Plan in
the form of sum assured and accrued bonuses, as we waive off all the future premiums in the IndiaFirst Maha Jeevan Plan.
Kiara’s education and dreams are all safeguarded with the IndiaFirst Life Waiver of Premium Rider.
