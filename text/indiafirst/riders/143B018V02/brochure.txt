5. What are the options available under this policy?
There are two rider options in the policy which the Master Policyholder/
Member can choose at the inception of the cover commencement:
a. Spouse Cover Benefit: In the event of death of the spouse during the term of
   the rider, the beneficiary would receive a lump sum benefit equal to rider Sum
   Insured. The Spouse cover in the policy is limited to a maximum of 50% of
   Member’s Death benefit cover in the base policy.
b. Term Rider Benefit: In the event of the death of the member during the term of
   the rider, if opted, the beneficiary would receive a lump sum benefit equal to
   rider sum Insured. Rider Sum Insured for Term Rider cannot exceed the
   compulsory Death benefit cover in the base policy.

6. What is the sum insured in this policy?
The sum insured in the policy will be as decided by you or your member as per the
need. However, the minimum sum insured would be the base plan’s minimum
sum insured or Rs. 10,000, whichever is lower of the two.
Maximum rider Sum Assured will be as per approved underwriting policy.
