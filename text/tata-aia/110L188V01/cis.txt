  Twin Booster shall comprise of three components: Loyalty
  Booster, Growth Booster, and Wealth Booster.

  1.     Loyalty Booster

  Starting from the 11th policy year, at the end of each policy month,
  the mortality charge (excluding underwriting extra and taxes)
  deducted in the 120th month prior shall be provided in the form of
  Loyalty booster. For instance, in the 121st policy month, the
  mortality charges deducted (excluding underwriting extra and
  taxes) in the 1st policy month shall be provided. Such booster
  shall continue till attained age 85 or till end of policy term,
  whichever is earlier.

  2.     Growth Booster


  The Mortality Charges and Premium Allocation Charges
  deducted, after adjusting for any amount already credited back
  through return of charges, loyalty boosters, and any cover
  continuance booster paid under the policy, shall be accumulated
  notionally from the date of deduction till the Maturity Date, at a
  rate of growth linked to the movement in BSE India 10 Year
  Sovereign Bond Index (updated on website from time to time),
  computed at the last business day of prior month. Such
  accumulation is notional and does not represent any segregated
  fund or unit holding.

  The applicable index and periodic growth rate used for
  determining such accumulation shall be published on the
  Company's website and shall be updated periodically and/or upon
  any change thereto.

  Upon maturity of the policy, the accumulated value shall be
  payable to the policyholder, subject to policy being in-force and
  all due premiums paid.

  The Growth Booster is not guaranteed and will vary with the
  movement in the specified index. Where the amount computed
  above is nil or negative, the Growth Booster payable shall be nil,
  and no amount shall be recoverable from the policyholder. In case
  of unavailability of the above Index i.e. BSE India 10 Year
  Sovereign Bond Index in future the Company may change the
  index to some other index by duly notifying the Customer 15 days
  in advance.
