       Surrender Benefits:
  During Lock-in period of 5 years:
  On complete withdrawal/surrender of the Policy, surrender value
  i.e. the total Fund Value [including any single premium fund
  value/regular premium fund value (as applicable) and Top-Up
  premium fund value] less applicable discontinuance charges as
  on the date of discontinuance shall be credited to the
  ‘Discontinued Policy Fund’ as maintained by the Company. The
  Proceeds of the Discontinued Policy shall be payable to You after
  the Lock-in-period. All benefits in this Policy shall cease on the
  date of complete withdrawal/surrender.

  In case of death of the Life Insured during the Lock-in-period, the
  “Proceeds of the Discontinued Policy” shall be payable to the
  claimant immediately.

  “Proceeds of the Discontinued Policy” means the Fund Value as
  on the Date of Discontinuance plus entire income earned after
  deduction of the Fund Management Charges, subject to a
  minimum guarantee of interest @ 4% p.a. or as prescribed by
  IRDA of India from time to time.

  Post Lock-in period of 5 years:
  Upon complete withdrawal/surrender of the Policy, the surrender
  value i.e., the total fund value [including any single premium fund
  value/regular premium fund value (as applicable) and Top-Up
  premium fund value] as on the date of complete withdrawal /
  surrender shall be payable.
