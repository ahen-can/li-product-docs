HOW DOES THIS PLAN WORK?

Here are the steps to make your Policy.
Step 1: Choose the Plan Option, Maturity Age, Premium Payment Term (PPT) and Premium Payment Mode.
Step 2: Choose to enhance your cover with Policy Continuance Benefit and/or any additional riders, as per your needs.
Step 3: Choose how you want to receive your income stream: Annual mode or Monthly mode, in Arrears or in Advance.
Step 4: Choose your Premium amount, basis which your Sum Assured on Maturity (SAM) and Sum Assured at Death
(SAD) will be calculated. Or you may directly choose your Sum Assured on Maturity (SAM) basis which your Sum
Assured at Death (SAD) and Premium amount shall be calculated.
Step 5: Choose if you would like to accrue survival benefits at inception or at anytime during the policy term.
Step 6: Secure your family with a Life Cover and income benefits associated with your plan customized as per your
needs.

BONUSES OFFERED
Your policy is eligible for bonuses during policy term. Bonuses represent your share in profits of the company’s
participating fund. Bonuses are, therefore, not guaranteed and are based on performance of the participating fund. Bonus
rate is paid as a percentage of the ‘Sum Assured on Maturity’ in the policy, and will be declared at the end of financial
year. This plan offers the following bonuses:
A. Annual Cash Bonus
Cash Bonus, if declared is a bonus rate (as a percentage rate applied on policy Sum Assured on Maturity) that may be
declared and is payable starting from:
         The second policy year in case of “Early Income” and “Early Income with Guaranteed Money Back” Plan
          Options
         The policy year PPT+2 in case of “Deferred Income” Plan Option
You can choose to take your cash bonuses (if declared), in either annual or monthly mode, in Arrears or in Advance.
Please refer to the section “Survival Benefit Payout Frequency” for more details.
B. Terminal Bonus
If declared, all eligible policies shall receive the terminal bonus on maturity or on exit due to death or surrender based
on the terminal bonus rates declared by the company. Terminal Bonus is an additional bonus paid only ONCE, on earlier
of death, surrender or maturity. In case of PCB no terminal bonus is payable at the time of death as the policy benefits
continues. The terminal bonus may be available from fifth policy year onwards. A Policy is eligible for Terminal Bonus
only once it has been in force for at least 4 years from Date of Commencement of Risk and all due premiums for the
first 4 policy years are paid.
The value of terminal bonus can vary for death, surrender and maturity.

A. ACCRUAL OF SURVIVAL BENEFITS
This plan gives you the flexibility to accrue your Survival Benefits - both the Guaranteed Income as well as the Cash
bonus (if declared) anytime during the Policy Term. Also, you may choose to cancel or withdraw your accrued benefits
completely or partially, at any time during the policy term. This option is subject to the following.
    In order to opt in or opt-out of this option, you would have to submit a request to the company 30 days before the
     policy anniversary and will get in-force from the subsequent policy anniversary. However, any change done to the
     payout will be applicable for a minimum of 1 policy year.
    The accrued survival benefits will be accumulated at an interest rate equal to the RBI Repo Rate published by RBI
     on its website and this rate will be reviewed annually. The ‘RBI Repo Rate’ for the financial year ending 31st March
     (every year) will be considered. As the interest rate will be reviewed at the beginning of each financial year, any
     change in this interest rate will be applicable from 1st May to 30th April. The current Repo Rate, as applicable on
     31st March 2024 is 6.50% p.a.
    You may withdraw the accrued survival benefits partly/fully any time during the policy term. The balance amount
     will keep accumulating as per the applicable terms and conditions and accrued survival benefit (if any) shall be
     payable along with benefits at the time of termination of the policy on death or maturity or surrender.
    In case you had opted for PCB or WOP rider at inception, the option to accrue the benefits shall be available with
     your nominee even after death of the life insured.
    Whenever you opt for accruing your benefits, your payout frequency would automatically be changed to annual,
     irrespective of the payment frequency chosen earlier. However, upon cancellation of accrual, you may choose to
     avail your payments monthly or annually.
    The ‘survival benefits and cash bonuses’, if opted by the policyholder to accrue, will continue to form part of ‘Life
     Participating fund’.

    Provided that the insurer shall have to communicate in writing to the insured or the legal representatives or

    Explanation I – For the purposes of this sub-section, the expression “fraud” means any of the following acts
