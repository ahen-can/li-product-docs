      Equity Derivative for Hedging Purposes:
      While creating a diversified portfolio helps reduce stock-specific risks, to protect portfolio returns from
      systematic risks, the funds may resort to hedging through Index/Stock Futures or Index/Stock Options as
      stipulated by IRDAI. This helps reduce market risk and volatility for policyholders. We will be complying
      to the exposure limits as prescribed by IRDAI guidelines.

      of Nifty MidCap.
