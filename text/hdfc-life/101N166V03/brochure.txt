        of regular income depending on the option chosen.

        Presenting HDFC Life Smart Income Plan

        ‘HDFC Life Smart Income Plan’ is an individual non-linked, participating savings life insurance
        plan that provides Guaranteed Income Benefit during the income payout term and Guaranteed
        Death Benefit during the term of the policy. It offers two benefit options – Enhanced Maturity and
        Enhanced Income - to choose from at the inception of the policy and depending on the option
        chosen, this plan may also provide Guaranteed Survival Benefit during the policy term in addition
        to the Maturity Benefit at the end of the policy term.
