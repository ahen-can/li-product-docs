      • The Daily Hospitalisation Cash Cover amount per day shall be 1% percentage of the Rider

          throughout the Rider Term.
      • Daily Hospitalisation Cash Cover is payable with a maximum limit of 20 days per Policy Year.
      • The specified number of days per Policy Year is inclusive of days hospitalised in non-ICU and
          ICU.
      • The Daily Hospitalisation Cash Cover will cease on the earlier of (i) claiming total of 100 days
          of Hospitalization or (ii) claiming 100% of Rider Sum Assured or (iii) at the end of Rider Term.
