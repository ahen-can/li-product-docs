       a) If the Insured dies when this Rider is in force, then in addition to any amounts payable under the Base Plan, We will

3)   Rider Duration

       This Rider will immediately and automatically terminate on the earliest of:
               i)     The Policy Anniversary coinciding with or immediately following the Insured attaining Age 70.

4)   Exclusions
