. Surrender Benefit:

Surrender Value (SV) is available only under Deferred Annuity Options and Annuity
Options with Refund of Full Purchase Price. The Policyholder can surrender the policy
at any time during Policy Term. The surrender value payable will be higher of
Guaranteed Surrender value (GSV) and Special Surrender Value (SSV).

18

On payment of Surrender Value the policy will be terminated.

Guaranteed Surrender Value (GSV) for Deferred Annuity Options within Deferment
Period:

GSV = GSV Factor A X Purchase Price + GSV Factor B X Accrued Guaranteed Additions

Guaranteed Surrender Value (GSV) for immediate annuity with Return of Purchase
Price and for deferred annuity post deferment period:

GSV = GSV Factor A X Total Premiums Paid - Annuity benefits already paid
The maximum SSV shall be restricted to the Death Benefit.

The SSV factors and thus the Special Surrender Value (SSV) are not guaranteed and
will be reviewed annually based on the prevailing yield on 10 Year G-Sec and
underlying experience. Any change in methodology used to determine the Special
Surrender Value (SSV) Factors shall be subject to prior appropriate approval in
accordance with IRDAI Regulations.

If an annuity policy is taken from NPS corpus, then surrender or cancellation of such
policy shall be subject to specific guidelines issued by PFRDA from time to time.

For more details on surrender benefit kindly refer to the policy document.

a) No policy of life insurance shall be called in question on any ground whatsoever after

called in question at any time within three years from the date of the policy, on the
ground of fraud or on the ground that any statement of or suppression of a fact

proposal or other document on the basis of which the policy was issued or revived or
rider issued. The insurer shall have to communicate in writing to the insured or the legal

fact or that such mis-statement or suppression are within the knowledge of the

In case of repudiation of the policy on the ground of misstatement or suppression of a

the date of repudiation shall be paid.

Nothing in this section shall prevent the insurer from calling for proof of age at any time

because the terms of the policy are adjusted on subsequent proof that the age of the
