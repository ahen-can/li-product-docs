13. Exclusions for Accidental Death Benefit (AD Benefit):
13.1. The AD Benefit shall not be paid in respect of deaths due to the consequences of or occurring during the events as
      specified below:
      13.1.1. Infection: Death caused or contributed to by any infection, except infection caused by an external visible
              wound accidentally sustained.
      13.1.2. Drug Abuse: The Life Assured being under the influence of alcohol or solvent abuse or use of drugs except
              under the direction of a registered Medical Practitioner.
      13.1.3. Self-inflicted Injury: Intentional self-inflicted Injury including the Injuries arising out of attempted suicide.
      13.1.4. Criminal acts: The Life Assured’s involvement in criminal and/or unlawful acts with criminal or unlawful
              intent.
      13.1.5. War and Civil Commotion: War, invasion, hostilities, (whether war is declared or not), civil war, rebellion,
              revolution, act of foreign enemy, armed or unarmed truce, mutiny, rebellion, strikes or taking part in a riot or
              civil commotion.
      13.1.6. Services: The Life Assured taking part in any naval, military or air force operation during peace time or
              during service in any police, paramilitary or any similar organization.
      13.1.7. Nuclear Contamination: The radioactive, explosive or hazardous nature of nuclear fuel materials or property
              contaminated by nuclear fuel materials or Accident arising from such nature.
      13.1.8. Aviation: The Life Assured’s participation in any flying activity, other than as a passenger in a commercially
              licensed aircraft.
      13.1.9. Hazardous sports and pastimes: The Life Assured engaging in or taking part in professional sport(s) or any
              hazardous pursuits, including but not limited to, diving or riding or any kind of race, underwater activities
              involving the use of breathing apparatus or not, martial arts, hunting, mountaineering, parachuting or bungee-
              jumping.

14. Exclusions for Accidental Partial Permanent Disability Benefit (APPD):
14.1. The APPD Benefit shall not be paid in respect of any disablement of the Life Assured occurring directly or indirectly
      as a result of (any of the following):
      14.1.1. Self-inflicted Injury: Intentional self-inflicted Injury including the injuries arising out of attempted suicide.
      14.1.2. Drug Abuse: The Life Assured being under the influence of alcohol or solvent abuse or use of drugs except
               under the direction of a registered Medical Practitioner.
      14.1.3. War and Civil Commotion: War, invasion, hostilities, (whether war is declared or not), civil war, rebellion,
               revolution, act of foreign enemy, armed or unarmed truce, mutiny, rebellion, strikes or taking part in a riot or
               civil commotion.
      14.1.4. Services: The Life Assured taking part in any naval, military or air force operation during peace time or
               during service in any police, paramilitary or any similar organization.
      14.1.5. Aviation: The Life Assured’s participation in any flying activity, other than as a passenger in a commercially
               licensed aircraft.
      14.1.6. Criminal acts: The Life Assured’s involvement in criminal and/or unlawful acts with criminal or unlawful
               intent.
      14.1.7. Any Injury incurred before the Date of Commencement of the Rider.
      14.1.8. Hazardous sports and pastimes: The Life Assured engaging in or taking part in professional sport(s) or any
               hazardous pursuits, including but not limited to, diving or riding or any kind of race, underwater activities
               involving the use of breathing apparatus or not, martial arts, hunting, mountaineering, parachuting, or bungee-
               jumping.
      14.1.9. Nuclear Contamination: the radio-active, explosive or hazardous nature of nuclear fuel materials or property
               contaminated by nuclear fuel materials or accident arising from such nature.
