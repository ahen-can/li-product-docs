Why SBI Life - Smart Invest Builder?
  You aim to move forward in life and achieve your goals, step by step. With SBI Life – Smart Invest Builder, a monthly contribution
  can go a long way in securing your family’s ﬁnancial protection while steadily building your wealth through market-linked returns.

  •   Protection: In case of any eventuality, ﬁnancial needs of the family will be taken care-of
  •   Long Term Wealth Creation: Goal based savings can be done by getting dual beneﬁts of Guaranteed Additions*of up to 150% of
      annualized premium and Market Linked Returns
  •   Get additional Guaranteed Additions on Maturity#
  •   No Premium Allocation Charge from 11th year onwards
  •   Flexibility: The following are available under the product
      o Choice of paying premiums regularly or for a limited period
      o Choice of 14 fund options and option to 'switch' between these funds.
      o Taking death beneﬁt as lumpsum or in periodic installments through ‘Settlement Option’. During the settlement period, the
         investment risk in the investment portfolio is borne by the beneﬁciary.
  •   Liquidity: Unlimited Partial Withdrawals^ from 6th policy year onwards
  •   Withdraw systematically: Avail ﬁxed monthly payouts for your supplementary income needs through Systematic Withdrawal
      Plan (SWP^), after 10th policy year.
  •   Option to enhance coverage by opting for SBI Life - Accident Beneﬁt Rider - Linked & SBI Life – Waiver of Premium Rider –
      Linked on payment of additional premium.
  •   Tax Savings**: As per prevailing norms under the Income Tax Laws
  *Guaranteed Additions at pre-speciﬁed percentage of Annualized Premium, would be added to the Fund Value at the end of 10th policy year and every 5th year thereafter,
  subject to policy being in-force as on date of Guaranteed addition. Longer the policy term, higher the guaranteed addition. Maximum guaranteed additions of 150% is
  available on selecting the policy term of 30 years under regular pay policies, provided the policy is inforce.
  #
   Guaranteed Addition on Maturity at pre-speciﬁed percentage (depending on the premium band) of Annualized Premium would be added to the Fund Value at the end of the
  Policy Term, subject to policy being in-force.

  ^Partial Withdrawals and SWP will not be applicable in case the Life Assured is Minor.

  ** Tax beneﬁts are as per Income Tax Laws & are subject to change from time to time. Please consult your Tax advisor for details.

Prohibition of Rebates:

   Section 41 of Insurance Act 1938, as amended from time to time, states:
   a) No person shall allow or offer to allow, either directly or indirectly, as an inducement to any person to take out or renew or
      continue an insurance in respect of any kind of risk relating to lives or property in India, any rebate of the whole or part of the
      commission payable or any rebate of the premium shown on the policy, nor shall any person taking out or renewing or continuing
      a policy accept any rebate, except such rebate as may be allowed in accordance with the published prospectus or tables of the
      insurer.
   b) Any person making default in complying with the provisions of this section shall be liable for a penalty which may extend to ten
      lakh rupees.
