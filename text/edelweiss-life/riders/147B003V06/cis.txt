           2.   Total and Permanent Disability

                We will not be liable to make any payment
                under this Rider Policy if Your disability is
                directly or indirectly due to or caused,
                occasioned, accelerated or aggravated by any
                of the following:

                   • Suicide or attempted suicide or self-
                     inflicted injury, whether You are
                     medically sane or insane.
                   • Failure to seek medical advice or
                     treatment.

                     enemy, hostilities, civil war, martial law,
