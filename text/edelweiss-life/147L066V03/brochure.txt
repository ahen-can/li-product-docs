become payable only after the completion of the lock-in period. After the lock-in period, the

such Policy during the discontinuance period.

discontinued, after addition of interest computed at the interest rate stipulated in the prevailing
regulations. The current minimum guaranteed interest rate applicable to the Discontinued Policy
Fund is 4 percent per annum.

B. Surrender of Policy after the lock-in period (after first five policy years):
The policyholder has an option to surrender the policy any time. Upon receipt of request for
surrender, the fund value as on date of surrender shall be payable and policy will terminate.
