                         • The initial Rider SA would be used for calculation of subsequent
                            minor CI claims as well, i.e. minimum of 25% of Rider SA or R5 lacs.
         HOW DOES THIS PLAN WORK?

EXAMPLE 1:

Mr. Bhatia, aged 35, renowned for his astute ﬁnancial planning and wise investment choices,
opted to invest in the ABSLI Wealth Inﬁnia Plan. He committed to paying an Annualized Premium
of R5,00,000 for 10 years, with the policy term extending over 20 years. Recognizing the
importance of mitigating risk and preparing for unforeseen medical expenses, he also added the
ABSLI Comprehensive Critical Illness Rider – Silver Option (covering 10 CIs), providing critical
illness coverage with a Sum Assured of R20,00,000 for an additional annual premium of just
R10,240.

Sadly, in the 14th year, Mr. Bhatia was diagnosed with End Stage Liver Failure, a major critical
illness. In this situation, he will receive the CI Rider Sum Assured of R20,00,000, and the rider will
end. However, the base policy beneﬁts stay intact and continue as per its terms.

EXAMPLE 2:

Mr. Singh, aged 35, decides to secure his ﬁnancial future by investing in the ABSLI Salaried
Suraksha Plan, with a Premium Paying Term of 6 years and a Policy Term of 30 years. He chooses
to pay an Annualized Premium of R10,00,000.

To enhance his coverage, Mr. Singh opts for the Platinum Option (covering 64 CIs) of the ABSLI
Comprehensive Critical Illness Rider, offering a Sum Assured of R1,00,00,000, for an additional
annual premium of just R1,96,500.

In the 20th year, Mr. Singh receives a diagnosis of Severe Osteoporosis, classiﬁed as a Minor
Critical Illness. Mr. Singh is entitled to receive the lesser of either 25% of the Critical Illness Rider
Sum Assured or R5,00,000. Since the minimum payout is R5,00,000, he receives that amount. As
a result, the Sum Assured of his Critical Illness Rider decreases to R95,00,000 (1,00,00,000 –
5,00,000).

During the same year, Mr. Singh is diagnosed with End Stage Lung Failure, categorized as a Major
Critical Illness. In this instance, he receives the remaining Critical Illness Rider Sum Assured of
R95,00,000, and the rider subsequently terminates.

   TERMINATION OF POLICY

The rider cover shall terminate upon the happening of the ﬁrst of the following events:
   1. the date of payment of freelook cancellation amount under the Base Policy or Rider;
   2. death of Life Insured;
   3. on payment of 100% rider SA under this rider;
   4. on the expiry of the rider coverage term;
   5. on date on which the rider or base cover is cancelled or terminated for any reason;
   6. the date of payment of the surrender value, if any;
   7. on cancellation/ termination of the cover by the Company on grounds of misrepresentation,
       fraud or nondisclosure subject to section 45 of the Insurance Act, 1938 as amended from
       time to time.
