                                                                        Critical Illness Benefit
                                                                        The benefit amount is payable if the Life Insured is
                                                                        diagnosed to be suffering from any one of the covered
                                                                        critical illness under this rider and survives for a period of
                                                                        at least 30 days following the date of diagnosis.

                                                                        The diagnosis must occur while this rider is in effect and
                                                                        the benefit amount shall equal the rider sum assured
                                                                        payable upon first occurrence of:
                                                                        (a) First Heart Attack of specified severity;
                                                                        (b) Cancer of specified severity;
                                                                        (c) Stroke resulting in permanent symptoms; or
                                                                        (d) Major Organ / Bone Marrow Transplant.

                                                                        MULTIPLE CLAIMS
                                                                        We will pay only one claim under this rider for an amount
                                                                        equal to 100% of the rider sum assured.
