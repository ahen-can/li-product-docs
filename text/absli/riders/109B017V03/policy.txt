RIDER BENEFIT PROVISIONS
Waiver of Premium Benefit
A claim can be submitted under this rider while it is in effect
and upon the first occurrence of the following events:
(a) the policy owner suffers a total and permanent
     disability lasting at least 180 days; or
(b) the policy owner is diagnosed to be suffering from any
     one of the covered critical illness under this rider and
     survives for a period of at least 30 days following the
     date of diagnosis; or
(c) upon the unfortunate death of the policy owner,
     provided the Life Insured under the base policy is a
     different person.

The covered critical illnesses under this rider are: First Heart
Attack of specified severity; Cancer of Specified Severity;
Stroke Resulting in Permanent Symptoms; and Major Organ
/ Bone Marrow Transplant as have been defined in Part B of
this document.
In case the rider is attached to a non-linked base policy, the
benefits as given in the Base Policy Contract and all riders
to which this rider is attached shall remain attached with
the policy and no future premiums to be paid by the
Policyholder.

In case the rider is attached to a unit-linked base policy,
ABSLI shall pay all the future premiums of the base policy
and for all the riders to which this rider is attached.

Premiums due prior to our acceptance of the claim shall be
payable by you and not waived by us.

In case of a total and permanent disability, we reserve the
right to verify the subsistence of the disability at any time
while we are waiving premiums. We may from time to time
require the policy owner to furnish proof of continued total
and permanent disability, failing which the premiums from
this point onwards shall no longer be waived by us and be
payable by you.
