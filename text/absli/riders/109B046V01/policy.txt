Revival:
The Policyholder has an option to revive this Rider Contract within the revival period subject to the revival
of the Base Policy Contract.

Surrender Benefit
Surrender Value is available upon receipt of 100% of total rider premiums as payable under the Rider.
The Surrender benefit for the rider where 100% rider premium is received will be as following:

35% × Total Rider Premiums Paid * (Outstanding Rider Term) / (Original Rider Term)

Where,
Total Rider Premiums Paid means total of all the rider premiums received, excluding any underwriting
extra premium, any loadings for modal premiums if any, and excluding taxes.

Outstanding Rider Term is calculated as the number of whole years from the date of surrender to the end
of the rider term.

Original Rider Term will be the Rider Term as defined in the Base Policy Schedule

If the rider is surrendered, without the base plan being surrendered, then surrender value on rider benefit
is available upon receipt of all premiums under the rider policy.

Non-Forfeiture Benefit/Reduced Paid up benefit:

discontinuance without any paid-up value. Further, lapsation of Base Policy Contract will result into
lapsation of Rider Policy.
