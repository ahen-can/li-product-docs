Accidental Death means death –
- which is caused by injury resulting from an accident and
- which occurs due to the said injury solely, directly and independently of any other causes and
- which occurs within 180 days of the occurrence of such accident but before the expiry of the cover and
- is not a result from any of the causes listed in the exclusions for accidental death benefit plus rider

Injury means accidental physical bodily harm excluding illness or disease solely and directly caused by external, violent and visible and evident
means which is verified and certified by a medical practitioner.

Accident is a sudden, unforeseen and involuntary event caused by external, visible and violent means.
