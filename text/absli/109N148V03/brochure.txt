    HOW DOES THE PLAN WORK ?
You shall be entitled to life cover for the chosen sum assured, at the inception of the Policy. The
premium payment term and policy term once selected cannot be changed at a later date.
Premium payable will vary depending upon the policy term and sum assured chosen.

Let's explore how does the plan work.
Under this plan, In the unfortunate event of the death of the Life Insured, anytime during the
Policy Term, the Nominee(s)/legal heir(s)/assignee will be paid Sum Assured on Death chosen at
Inception in Lump Sum. provided all due premiums have been paid.
   • Let us take the example of Mr. Sharma, 30 years old, who opts for ABSLI Insta Digi Term
      Plan for a Sum Assured of R1 crore. He chooses a Policy Term of 30 years and Premium
      Payment Term of 10 years.
Unfortunately, he dies during the 25th Policy year. His Nominee is paid a lump-sum equal to R1
crore and the Policy terminates. The same is illustrated below:
