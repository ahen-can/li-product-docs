          - On first death of either of the Annuitants, 100% of the Annuity shall continue to be paid as long as one of

          - On death of the last surviving Annuitant, the Annuity will cease immediately and the Purchase Price shall

    paid from the Death Benefit payable under this Policy.

      i. Option 1: Life Annuity with Return of 100% of Purchase Price (ROP):

           A fixed Annuity, as specified in the Policy Schedule, shall be payable, till either of the Annuitants is alive.

2.1 The Annuity shall be payable in arrears at the end of chosen Annuity Payment Frequency from the date of
    purchase of the plan.

2.2 The Annuity Option chosen at policy inception, cannot be changed thereafter.

        Annuitant, it can be availed by the Secondary Annuitant.

        interest amount payable on loan does not exceed 50% of the Annual Annuity amount payable under the
        Policy.
     d) The interest on Loan shall be at 10-year G-Sec rate per annum as at 1st April of the relevant financial year,
        as published by M/s. FBIL, plus 200 bps and shall be applicable for all loans granted during the period of
        twelve months, beginning 1st May of the relevant financial year.
     e) The compound interest rate applicable as on May 1st, 2024 is 9.23%% p.a. Any change in basis of
        determination of interest rate for Policy Loan can be done only after prior approval of IRDAI.
     f) The Loan interest will be recovered from the Annuity amount payable under the Policy.
     g) The Loan interest will accrue as per the frequency of Annuity payment under the Policy and it will be due
        on the date of Annuity.
     h) The Loan outstanding shall be recovered from the claim proceeds under the Policy.
