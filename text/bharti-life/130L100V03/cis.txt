                          Subject to the Policy being in-force, and the Variant chosen, Loyalty
                          Additions (as a % of Policy Fund Value) will be credited to the Policy at the
                          end of each Policy Year starting from the end of tenth Policy Year till one
                          year before the date of maturity. The Loyalty Additions will be payable along
                          with the Policy Fund Value on Maturity (in the form of additional units).

                          Wealth Booster:

                          Subject to the Policy being in-force, Wealth Booster (as a % of Policy Fund
                          Value) will be credited to the Policy at the end of fifth policy year and at
                          Maturity date (in the form of additional units). The Wealth Booster will be
                          payable along with the Policy Fund Value at Maturity

                          Return of Mortality Charges (RoMC) and Return of Premium Allocation
                          Charge (RoPAC) (Applicable only in Growth Variant):

                          Subject to Policy being in force, the total amount of Mortality charges
                          (excluding any extra Mortality charge & or any applicable tax/cess levied)
                          and total Premium Allocation Charges (excluding any applicable tax/cess
                          levied) deducted during the Policy Term will be credited to the Policy at
                          maturity in the form of additional units.
