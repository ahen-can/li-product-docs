Introduction

to do but never had the time for. While the income may slow down, aspirations don’t. Daily expenses, healthcare needs, and
unexpected situations continue, making financial preparedness an important part of a comfortable retirement.

years, you can create a financial cushion that supports your lifestyle and reduces dependence on others after retirement.
Generali Central Sunehra Pension Plan is designed with this objective in mind. It helps you save systematically, provides life cover
during the policy term, and enables you to build a retirement corpus that can be used to secure a steady income in your post
retirement years.

                                   What are your benefits?
1. Vesting / Maturity benefit
On survival of the Life Assured till the vesting date and on payment of all due premiums, the vesting benefit payable shall be
the higher of:
1. Sum Assured on Vesting plus Accrued Reversionary Bonus plus Terminal Bonus (if declared)
2. Assured Benefit on Vesting
Where,
“Annualized Premium” shall be the premium amount payable in a year excluding taxes, rider premiums, underwriting extra
premiums and loadings for modal premiums.
“Assured Benefit on Vesting” is equal to 105% of Total premiums paid.
“Sum Assured” is equal to PPT * Annualized Premium.
“Sum Assured on Vesting” is equal to Sum Assured.
On vesting, the policyholder can exercise any one of the following options:
1. To utilize the entire proceeds to purchase annuity from the same insurer at the then prevailing annuity rate.
2. To commute up to 60% and utilize the balance amount to purchase annuity from the same insurer at the then prevailing
   annuity rate.
3. The policyholder shall have an option to purchase annuity from another insurer at the then prevailing rate to the extent of
   percentage, as stipulated by the Authority, currently 50%, of the entire proceeds of the policy net of commutation.
In case the proceeds of the policy on vesting are not sufficient to purchase minimum annuity, such proceeds may be paid to the
policyholder/beneficiary as lumpsum.

2. Death Benefit
On death of the life assured during the policy term, the death benefit shall be payable as mentioned below, provided that the
Policy has not Lapsed or been converted into a Reduced Paid-up Policy.
Death Benefit payable shall be higher of:
      1.     Assured Benefit on death plus Accrued Reversionary Bonus plus Interim/Terminal Bonus (if declared)
      2.     105% of Total Premiums Paid
Where,
"Total Premiums Paid" means total of all the premiums received, excluding any extra premium, any rider premium and taxes,
if collected explicitly.
“Assured Benefit on death” is equal to 101% of Total premiums paid.
On death of the policyholder, nominee can exercise any of the following options:
      1.     Use the full policy amount, or a part of it, to purchase an annuity at the prevailing rates at that time.
      2.     Withdraw the entire proceeds of the policy.
In case the proceeds of the policy on death are not sufficient to purchase minimum annuity, such proceeds may be paid to
the nominee/policyholder/beneficiary as lumpsum.

3. Bonus
This plan participates in the profits of the Participating Pension Policyholders’ fund of the Company during the policy term.
Compound Reversionary Bonus
Compound Reversionary Bonuses will be declared at the end of each financial year and shall be applicable to the policy from
the first policy year till the end of policy term, provided all due premiums have been paid. Compound Reversionary Bonuses
will be applied on the (Annualized Premium Paid plus accrued Compound Reversionary Bonus, if declared). The Reversionary
Bonus would depend on the actual experience with respect to the investment return, expenses, mortality, tax etc and would
be declared keeping in mind a long term view of expected future experience.
Terminal Bonus
Terminal bonus, if declared, will be paid on death or surrender during the policy term or on maturity of the policy. Terminal
bonus, if declared, shall be payable only after completion of first policy year.

You get a grace period of 30 days for yearly, half-yearly and quarterly Premium Payment frequencies and 15 days for monthly

Premium remains unpaid at the end of the Grace Period, the policy shall Lapse. For Single Pay, grace period is not applicable.

   interest rate applicable on loans is 9% p.a compounded half-yearly for Financial Year 2026-27. 10-year G-Sec rates are
   as declared by FIMMDA (Fixed Income Money Market And Derivatives Association Of India).
• Any change in the basis of determination of interest rate as given above for policy loan shall be done only after prior

• The minimum amount of policy loan that can be taken is Rs. 10,000/-.
• Any unpaid interest shall be added to the principal loan and bear interest at the prevalent rate.
• The policyholder has the option to repay the principal and accrued interest in full or any part of the loan during the term
  of the Policy.
• Any unpaid loan and interest on the Policy will be deducted from any payment or proceeds under the Policy at the time
  of settlement.
• The claim of the company for any loan including outstanding interest will have priority over the claim of any assignee.

• Interest shall be payable on the next Policy Anniversary or six months before the next Policy Anniversary if earlier, after
   the loan date; and every six months thereafter until the loan is repaid.
• Until such time as the loan persists in the company’s books, the policy shall remain assigned to the company.
• Policy loan will not be extended beyond the Maturity Date.
• Under circumstances when the loan amount along with outstanding interest exceeds the Surrender Value, the
  policyholder shall be intimated of the same and shall be given a period of three months to repay the outstanding dues.
  In the event of failure to repay by the required date, the policy will be terminated/foreclosed. This provision will not apply

4. Partial Withdrawal
• Partial withdrawals can be made only after the completion of 3 years from the date of commencement of the policy during
   the deferment period.
• Total partial withdrawal shall not exceed 25% of total premiums paid as on the date of partial withdrawal.
• The amount of partial withdrawal shall be treated as preponement of the commuted portion of the surrender / vesting
   benefit.
• Partial withdrawal shall not result into termination of the contract.
• Partial withdrawal shall not be used for the adjustment of sum assured on death.
• It shall be allowed only three times during the entire term of the policy.
• It shall be allowed only against the stipulated reasons:
 a. Higher education of children, including legally adopted child.
 b. Marriage of children, including legally adopted child.
 c. Purchase or construction of a residential house or flat in the Life Assured’s own name or in joint name with their legally
      wedded spouse. However, if the Life assured already owns a residential house or flat (other than ancestral property),
      no withdrawal shall be permitted.
 d. For treatment of critical illnesses of self, spouse or dependent children, including legally adopted child.
 e. Medical and incidental expenses arising from disability or incapacitation suffered by the life assured.
 f. Expenses incurred by the life assured for skill development/reskilling or any other self-development activities.
 g. Expenses incurred by the Life assured for the establishment of her/his own venture or any start-ups.
5. Paid-up
If due premiums for the first or more policy years have been paid and any subsequent premium is not paid within the grace
period, the policy will be converted into a paid-up policy.
If a policy is converted into a paid-up policy, it will not accrue any future reversionary bonuses. The bonuses already accrued,
remains attached to the policy. The paid-up sum assured is calculated as follows:
Paid-Up Sum Assured = Sum Assured x (Number of Premiums Paid / Total number of premiums payable)
Benefits under Paid Up policy
     • Paid Up Death Benefit:
     Paid Up Death benefit payable shall be higher of:
        i.      Assured Benefit on death plus Accrued Reversionary Bonus plus Interim/Terminal Bonus (if declared)
        ii.     105% of Total Premiums Paid
     • Paid Up Maturity Benefit:
     Paid Up Maturity Benefit payable; shall be higher of:
        i.      Paid-Up Sum Assured plus Accrued Reversionary Bonus plus Interim/Terminal Bonus (if declared)
        ii.     Assured Benefit on Vesting
The policyholder has the option to revive the policy within five years from the due date of first unpaid premium. A paid-up
policy cannot be revived once the policy term is over.
The policyholder has the option to surrender a paid-up policy any time before the end of the policy term.
If the Paid-up Sum assured (exclusive of Compound Reversionary Bonuses) is less than two thousand five hundred rupees,
the Policy may be terminated after expiry of revival period by paying the surrender value.
6. Surrender
For Single premium, the policy shall immediately acquire Surrender Value on payment of due premium.
For Limited Premium Payment Term policies with a premium payment term of less than 5 years, the policy shall acquire a
Surrender Value immediately upon receipt of the first full year’s premium.
For Regular/Limited premium policies with a premium payment term of greater than or equal to 5 years, the policy shall
acquire a Surrender Value after completion of first policy year provided one full year premium has been received.
The policyholder may terminate the policy any time before the end of policy term by surrendering the policy for a surrender
value. On surrender, the higher of Guaranteed Surrender Value (GSV) and Special Surrender Value (SSV) will be paid.
On surrender, the policyholder can exercise any one of the following options:
    • To utilize the entire proceeds to purchase annuity from the same insurer at the then prevailing annuity rate.
    • To commute up to 60% and utilize the balance amount to purchase annuity from the same insurer at the then
        prevailing annuity rate.
    • The policyholder shall have an option to purchase annuity from another insurer at the then prevailing rate to the extent
        of percentage, as stipulated by the Authority, currently 50%, of the entire proceeds of the policy net of commutation.
Surrender Value = Max (GSV, SSV)
Guaranteed Surrender Value (GSV):
Guaranteed Surrender Value = Guaranteed Surrender Value Factor for Premium x Total Premiums paid + Guaranteed
Surrender Value Factor for Vested Bonus x (Vested Compound Reversionary Bonus, if declared).
Special Surrender Value (SSV):
Special Surrender Value will be equal to:
For premium paying and reduced paid-up policies:
Special Surrender Value Factor for Premiums x Total Premiums Paid + Special Surrender Value Factor for Vested
Bonus x (Vested Compound Reversionary Bonus)
For fully paid-up policies (i.e. policies where premiums due till premium payment term has been paid in full):
Special Surrender Value Factor for Premiums x Total Premiums Paid + Special Surrender Value Factor for Vested
Bonus x Vested Compound Reversionary Bonus + Special Surrender Value Factor for Contingent Future Revisionary
Bonus x Contingent Future Revisionary Bonus + Terminal bonus Bonus (if any.)
Special Surrender Value (SSV) shall be based on the Company’s expectation of future financial and demographic conditions
and may be reviewed annually by the company in accordance with the IRDAI regulations. The Special Surrender Value shall
be in compliance with Clause 4 (A) (a) (4) of Schedule I of the IRDAI (Insurance Products) Regulations, 2024.
The Surrender Value shall be adjusted for any partial withdrawals already paid.
In case the proceeds of the policy either on surrender are not sufficient to purchase minimum annuity as defined in
Regulation 3(a) of IRDAI (Minimum Limits for Annuities and Other Benefits) Regulations, 2015, as amended from time to time,
such proceeds may be paid to the nominee/policy holder/beneficiary as lumpsum.
The policy terminates on surrender, and no further benefits are payable under the policy.
For more details on the surrender benefit, please refer to the policy document.
