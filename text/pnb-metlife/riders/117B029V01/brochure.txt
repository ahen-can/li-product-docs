  What is PNB MetLife Group Accidental Benefit Rider
PNB MetLife Group Accidental Benefit Rider is a Group Non-Linked, Non-Participating, Pure Risk,
Health Insurance Rider. This rider provides accident-related protection to eligible members covered
under the group policy.
In the event of an accidental death or disability, the rider provides an additional benefit equal to the
Sum Assured under the selected benefit option. The rider must be attached to the Base Master Policy
and serves to enhance the overall insurance protection available under the policy.

                    ADB plus ATPD: both accidental death and accidental total and permanent disability

    Rider Benefits
The Rider offers 3 types of Benefit options as mentioned below. Only one option can be chosen at the
inception and cannot be changed during the term of the rider.

Death Benefit
Plan Option 1: Accidental Death Benefit
In the event of the Accidental Death of the Insured Member during the Rider Policy Term under the chosen
benefit option, 100% of the Rider Sum Assured (up to a maximum of three times the Base Policy Sum Assured,
as selected) as shown in the Cover Schedule in the Certificate of Insurance, shall be payable, provided the rider
is in force on the date of the Accident and the death occurs within 180 days from the date of the Accident.
For Accidental Death occurring after the Rider Policy Term, the benefit will still be payable, provided the death
occurs within 180 days from the date of the Accident. This is subject to the Accident having occurred during
the Rider Policy Term and the rider being in force on the date of the Accident.

Conditions Applicable:
 The Insured Member must have sustained bodily injury directly and solely from the Accident, caused
   by external, violent, and visible means, and such injury must be independent of all other causes.
 Death must occur within 180 days from the date of the Accident and must result solely, directly, and
   independently from such injury.
 The Accident leading to death must have occurred during the Rider Policy Term, even if the death occurs
   after the Rider Policy Term, provided it is within 180 days from the date of the Accident.
   Upon payment of the rider benefit, the Rider shall terminate.

Accident is defined as sudden, unforeseen and involuntary event caused by external, visible and violent
means.


Plan Option 2 : Accidental Total and Permanent Disability Benefit (ATPD)
 In the event, the Insured Member meets with an Accident during the Rider Policy Term that results in the
Insured Member being subject to one or more of the impairments specified as below, 100% of the Rider Sum
Assured (subject to a maximum of the Base Policy Sum Assured) applicable on the date of the insured event, as
shown in the Cover Schedule, shall be payable, provided the rider is in force on the date of the Accident and
such impairment occurs within 180 days from the date of the Accident and independent of all other causes.
The impairments are as follows:

    total and irrecoverable loss of sight of both eyes.
    amputation or loss of use, of both hands at or above the wrists or
    amputation or loss of use, of both feet at or above the ankles or
    amputation or loss of use, of one hand at or above the wrist and one foot at or above the ankle.

Loss of use of limbs means total and permanent loss of all functional use of a limb or organ.
Limb means the whole hand at or above the wrist or the whole foot at or above the ankle.
Loss of sight - means total, permanent and irreversible loss of all vision in both eyes as a result of accident. The
diagnosis must be clinically confirmed by an appropriate Medical Practitioner. The blindness must not be
correctable by aides or surgical procedures.
                                                                                                                   2
The Blindness is evidenced by:
i. corrected visual acuity being 3/60 or less in both eyes or;
ii. the field of vision being less than 10 degrees in both eyes.
On occurrence of ATPD due to Accident, ATPD benefit will be triggered if the Insured Member is unable to
perform three or more activities of daily living cause as defined either with or without the use of
mechanical equipment, special devices or other aids and adaptations in use for disabled persons.
Activities of Daily Living are:
Washing: the ability to wash in the bath or shower (including getting into and out of the bath or shower)
or wash satisfactorily by other means
Dressing: the ability to put on, take off, secure and unfasten all garments and, as appropriate, any
braces, artificial limbs or surgical appliances
Transferring: the ability to move from a bed or an upright chair or wheelchair and vice versa
Mobility: the ability to move indoors from room to room on level surfaces/ the ability to walk a distance
of 200m on a flat ground
Toileting: the ability to use the lavatory or otherwise manage the bowel and bladder functions so as to
maintain a satisfactory level or personal hygiene
Feeding: the ability to feed oneself once food has been prepared and made available


The disabilities must have continuously lasted, without interruption, for at least 180 days from the date
of onset of disability and must be certified as permanent by a panel of independent medical
practitioners. The ATPD benefit is payable only after the disability has continued without interruption for
at least 180 days and has been certified as permanent.
Rider terminates on payment of the rider benefit.


Plan Option 3: Accidental Death Benefit plus Accidental Total and Permanent Disability
Under this option, both Accidental Death Benefit (ADB) and Accidental Total and Permanent Disability (ATPD)
Benefits are available up to their respective Rider Sum Assureds throughout the Rider Policy Term. The
respective ADB and ATPD Sum Assured are subject to a maximum of the Base Policy Sum Assured.
In the event the Insured Member suffers an Accidental Total and Permanent Disability, the Rider coverage in
respect of the ATPD Benefit shall cease immediately upon payment of ATPD Benefit i.e. 100% of rider sum
assured as per the Cover Schedule in the Certificate of Insurance. Thereafter, the Rider shall continue only with
the Accidental Death Benefit (ADB) cover, and the coverage under the Base Master Policy shall remain
unaffected and continue as per its terms.
Upon the first occurrence of Accidental Death, the Rider shall terminate after payment of the Accidental Death
Benefit i.e. 100% of sum assured as per the Cover Schedule in the Certificate of Insurance as on date of death of
the Insured Member and no further Rider benefits shall be payable thereafter.

 Maturity Benefit
There is no Maturity Benefit Payable under this Rider Policy


 Rider Cover Options
The rider covers both Reducing and Level cover options.
Level Cover: Under this option, Sum Assured remains constant throughout the term i.e. absolute
amount assured chosen at inception of the cover.
Reducing Cover: Under this option, Sum Assured shall reduce during the term i.e. Absolute amount
assured as on the date of insured event(s) as specified in the cover schedule


 Rider Benefit Payout Options
On the occurrence of the insured event (death/disability) the policy proceeds would be paid out to the
nominee/insured member through any of the following two options:
Lump sum: On occurrence of any of the above-mentioned events, the benefit amount shall be payable
as lumpsum
Monthly Income: The outstanding loan amount on the date of the insured event will be paid as a lump
sum. Any benefit amount above the outstanding loan amount will be paid as equal monthly instalments
over a selected period of 24 to 120 months (2 to 10 years). The first monthly instalment will be paid one
month after the insured event.
If the monthly income per month comes out to be less Rs 5,000 per month, then the payout will be made
in lump sum only.
The Benefit Payout Option must be chosen at the inception of the cover and may not be changed
subsequently.


 Other Provisions
Moratorium Period
This option is available for those Insured Members who have opted for moratorium period on the
repayment of principal amount for a period of 3 months to 7 years on their loans.
During the moratorium period, the cover is determined as follows:
 In case of reducing cover, the Company shall cover the life from the date of loan for the period of
  moratorium (maximum period of seven years) for the entire cover amount if interest is paid by the
  Insured Member during moratorium period or the entire cover amount plus the interest if interest is
  accrued during moratorium period and thereafter the cover amount will be as per the Cover
  Schedule.
 In case of level cover with moratorium option: the cover will remain Level throughout the coverage
  term including the Moratorium period and will be equal to the Sum Assured chosen at inception of
  the policy.
The moratorium benefit has to be provided by the loan offering institution only. The moratorium option
once chosen cannot be altered at any time during the term of coverage under the base group policy.

Free Look Period
Master Policy Holder / Members of the group have a period of 30 days beginning from the date of
receipt of the Master Policy / Certificate of Insurance, whether received electronically or otherwise, to
review the terms and conditions of this Insurance Coverage. If the Master Policy Holder / Members have
any objections to any of the terms and conditions, then he / she can cancel the Master Policy /
Certificate of Insurance by giving a written notice to us, stating the reasons for the objections. Then
he/she shall be entitled to a refund of the premium paid subject to only a deduction of proportionate risk
premium for the period of cover, stamp duty charges and expenses towards medical examination (if any).
All rights of the member(s) under this Policy shall immediately stand extinguished at the cancellation of
the Master Policy / Certificate of Insurance.
The Free Look option shall not be available for the cases where Rider Policy Term/Rider Cover
Term, as applicable, is less than a year.

Surrender Benefit
This is a pure protection rider and has no surrender value. However, the Company will pay an Unexpired
Risk Premium Value if the Insured Member voluntarily terminates the cover during the Rider Cover Term,
calculated as shown below:
Unexpired Risk Premium = Value

                                            Total number of months         Rider Sum Assured in force
                                             remaining till maturity       at the time of Termination
                 X% of Total Rider     x    Total number of months
                                                                       x   Rider Sum Assured at the
                 Premiums Paid
                                            in the Rider Cover Term        inception of the coverage
Where,
a) X = 30 if terminated anytime within 3 years from the commencement of the coverage,
b) X = 50 if terminated anytime between 4th year to 10th year from the commencement of the coverage,
c) X = 70 if terminated after ten years from the commencement of the coverage.
In case the Master Policyholder surrenders the Rider, the Members of the group will be given the option to
continue life cover till the end of the Coverage Term. The option to continue the cover will be applicable
only to those schemes where the premium is paid by the members, provided the Member Base Policy is in
force.
In the event, the Members choose not to continue the Cover upon Surrender of the Master Base policy
along with the Rider chosen or on surrender of the Rider only by the Master Policyholder, then the
Unexpired Risk Premium Value, if any, will be payable in accordance with the Policy terms.
In case the loan is prepaid before the date of maturity (under both reducing cover and level cover options),
the applicable Unexpired Premium Value if any, will be payable to the Insured Member and the risk cover
terminates.


Policy Loan
Loan is not applicable under this Rider Policy


Termination of the Rider
The coverage shall terminate, upon occurrence of the earlier of the following event:
 When the coverage under the Master Base Policy/ Member Base policy (as applicable) to which the
   Rider Policy/ member level Rider is attached expires/terminates due to maturity, cancellation Surrender,
   death or due to any other reason; or
 Upon expiry of the Rider Cover Term for each of Rider Benefit Option(s) chosen; or
 On cancellation of the Rider Policy by the Company for any reason whatsoever; or
 On payment of Free Look cancellation proceeds; or
 The Insured Member’s death; or
 If the Insured Member ceases to satisfy any of the eligibility criteria or ceases to be an eligible member
   of the Group; or
 If the Insured Member expressly opts to discontinue the cover; or
 If the Member reaches Risk Ceasing Age; or
 Upon payment of the Rider Benefit to the Claimant/Beneficiary except for Option 3 where Rider will not
   terminate in case of payment of ATPD
