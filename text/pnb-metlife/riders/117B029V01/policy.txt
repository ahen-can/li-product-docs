3. PART C


RIDER FEATURES, RIDER BENEFITS & RIDER PREMIUM PAYMENT CONDITIONS
1. Rider Features
PNB MetLife Group Accidental Benefit Rider is a group, non-linked, non-participating, pure risk, health
insurance rider. This Rider Policy offers 3 (three) types of Rider Benefit options:
Option 1- Accidental Death Benefit (ADB),
Option 2- Accidental Total and Permanent Disability Benefit (ATPD) and
Option 3- Accidental Death plus Accidental Total and Permanent Disability Benefit (ADB plus ATPD)
as listed below in the Rider Benefit section. The Rider Benefits will be payable subject to the terms and
conditions of this Rider Policy.
Only 1 (one) of the above Rider Benefit Option can be selected at Rider inception and once selected it cannot
be changed later.


 2. Rider Benefits

2.1. Death Benefit

2.1.1. Option 1- Accidental Death Benefit (ADB)
     In the event of an Accidental Death of the Insured Member within the Rider Cover Term under this Rider
     Benefit Option, 100% of the Rider Sum Assured as per the Rider Cover Schedule as on the date of death
     shall be payable provided the Rider Benefit Option is In-Force on the date of Accident and death occurs
     within 180 (One Hundred and Eighty) days from the date of Accident.

 ⎯ For Accidental Death occurring beyond the Rider Cover Term, the Rider Benefit, 100% of the Rider Sum
   Assured as per the Rider Cover Schedule as on the date of death, shall be payable provided the death is
   within 180 (One Hundred and Eighty) days from the date of Accident. This is applicable subject to the
   Accident occurring within the Rider Cover Term and the Rider Benefit option being In-Force on the date
   of the Accident.

      Conditions Applicable –

  o    The Insured Member has sustained any Bodily Injury directly, solely from the Accident, caused by an
       external, violent and visible means independent of all other causes. Death arising from such Injury must
       occur within 180 (One Hundred and Eighty) days from the date of Accident.
  o    The Accident which eventually leads to death has occurred during the Rider Cover Term, even if the
       death occurs after Rider Cover Term but within 180 (One Hundred and Eighty) days of Accident.

       On death of the Insured Member due to Accident, the Rider Cover shall terminate and all rights, benefits
       and interests under this Rider Policy will stand extinguished.

 2.1.2. Option 2 - Accidental Total and Permanent Disability Benefit (ATPD)
    In the event, the Insured Member meets with an Accident during the Rider Cover Term that results in the
    Insured Member being subject to 1 (one) or more of the impairments specified as below, 100% of the
    Rider Sum Assured, as per the Rider Cover Schedule as applicable on the date of the Accident, shall be

    payable, provided the Rider Benefit Option is In-Force on the date of the Accident and such impairment
    occurs within 180 (One Hundred and Eighty) days from the date of the Accident and independent of all
    other causes.

    The Rider Benefit i.e., 100% of the Rider Sum Assured, under this Rider Benefit Option, shall be payable
    even where such impairment occurs beyond the Rider Cover Term, provided that the Accident occurred
    during the Rider Cover Term and such impairment occurs within 180 (One Hundred and Eighty) days
    from the date of the Accident.

    The impairments are as follows:
    • total and irrecoverable loss of sight of both eyes; or
    • amputation or loss of use, of both hands at or above the wrists; or
    • amputation or loss of use, of both feet at or above the ankles; or
    • amputation or loss of use, of one hand at or above the wrist and one foot at or above the ankle.

   Where,
a) Loss of use of limbs- means total and permanent loss of all functional use of a limb or organ as a result
   of Accident. Limb means the whole hand at or above the wrist or the whole foot at or above the ankle.

b) Loss of sight (Blindness) - means total, permanent and irreversible loss of all vision in both eyes as a
   result of Accident. The diagnosis of loss of sight must be clinically confirmed by an appropriate Medical
   Practitioner. The Blindness must not be correctable by aides or surgical procedures.
   The Blindness is evidenced by:
   i. corrected visual acuity being 3/60 or less in both eyes; or
   ii. the field of vision being less than 10 degrees in both eyes.

   On occurrence of ATPD , ATPD Benefit will be triggered if the Insured Member is unable to perform 3
   (Three) or more Activities of Daily Living as defined below, either with or without the use of mechanical
   equipment, special devices or other aids and adaptations in use for disabled persons.

    Activities of Daily Living are:

1. Washing: the ability to wash in the bath or shower (including getting into and out of the bath or shower)
   or wash satisfactorily by other means.
2. Dressing: the ability to put on, take off, secure and unfasten all garments and, as appropriate, any braces,
   artificial limbs or surgical appliances.
3. Transferring: the ability to move from a bed or an upright chair or wheelchair and vice versa.
4. Mobility: the ability to move indoors from room to room on level surfaces or the ability to walk a distance
   of 200 m on a flat ground.
5. Toileting: the ability to use the lavatory or otherwise manage the bowel and bladder functions so as to
   maintain a satisfactory level or personal hygiene.
6. Feeding: the ability to feed oneself once food has been prepared and made available.

The above Accidental Total and Permanent Disability must have lasted, without interruption, for at least 180
(One Hundred and Eighty) consecutive days and must be deemed permanent by a panel of independent Medical
Practitioners.

The ATPD coverage for Insured Member will terminate on payment of ATPD Benefit.

  next 24 months - 120 months i.e., 2 - 10 years. The first instalment of Monthly Income will be payable 1
