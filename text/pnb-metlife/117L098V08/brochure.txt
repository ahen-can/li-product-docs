     On survival of the Life Assured till the end of the policy term, provided the policy is in

     total fund value as on the maturity date.

     •     In case of settlement period after the Maturity date, the life insurance cover shall
           be maintained at 105% of the Total premiums paid. Accordingly, mortality charges
