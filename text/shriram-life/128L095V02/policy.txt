     The policyholder can opt this option to reduce the risk of investing the full premiums into fund(s) with

     selected option will become effective on the subsequent premiums. Further this option is not applicable
     during the settlement period. When an ATO is opted, partial withdrawal and switching between the
     funds is allowed except that the policyholder cannot switch to the Preserver fund.
