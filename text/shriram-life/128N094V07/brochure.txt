 Settlement Option

Settlement in Instalments The maturity beneﬁt is paid in four equal
annual instalments starting from the date of maturity. This can be
used to meet the planned expenses for a speciﬁc period like child’s

Settlement in lump sum If the life assured opts to receive the

buying a house or building a retirement corpus.
