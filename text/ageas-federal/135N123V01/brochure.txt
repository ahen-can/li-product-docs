Introduction
Retirement marks the beginning of a new life stage where financial
certainty becomes essential to maintaining independence and lifestyle.
With increasing life expectancy and evolving aspirations, relying solely
on savings may not be enough- creating the need for solutions that can
transform accumulated wealth into a predictable, lifelong income
stream while also protecting loved ones.


The Ageas Federal Life Insurance Retire Smart Plan is designed to address
this need through a flexible annuity framework that adapts to individual
goals. Offering a choice of immediate or deferred income, multiple
annuity    and    payout      options,    joint   life   coverage     and    the
return-of-purchase-price features, the plan enables you to structure
retirement income your way - backed by guaranteed lifelong payouts
and additional protection options.




Ageas Federal Life Insurance Retire Smart Plan will be hereafter referred to as ‘Retire Smart Plan’
throughout the document for ease of reference.
