                                   Claim Registration Process
                                   Claims are registered on receipt of the following documents
                                   as per policy terms and conditions. However, we can waive
                                   documents on case-to-case basis.
                                       • Death certificate
                                       • Claim form signed by the claimant
                                       • KYC and Bank details of the nominee
                                       • Original Policy Document
                                       • Certificate from physician/hospital last attended with
                                           all details of illness
                                       • A copy of medical records for diagnosis & treatment
                                           attested by treating doctor; Medical Reports to
                                           include initial Diagnosis / findings, further follow-up
                                           treatment report, medicine prescriptions from initial
                                           treatment till date, any other relevant medical report
                                           pertaining to the Illness
                                       • Medical certificate confirming the diagnosis of Critical
                                           Illness.
                                   The Claim intimation date is the date of receipt of
                                   mandatory documents at the corporate office or any of the
                                   Ageas Federal Branch Offices whichever is earlier.
