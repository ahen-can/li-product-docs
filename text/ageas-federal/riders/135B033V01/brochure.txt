                           How does the plan work


Ageas Federal Life Insurance Group Accidental Rider is a Non-Linked, Non-Participating, Group Pure Risk Premium, Life
Insurance Rider. This Rider will be attached only to the Non-Linked, Non-Participating Group products and shall bear the
nature and character of the base product to which it is attached.



Master Policyholder/Member can attach the Rider Cover to their Base Cover at:

     Inception of base product or,

     On anniversary of base product




Master Policyholder/Member will select the Rider Cover Term and Rider Premium Payment Term at inception of rider cover.

The following Rider Benefits are offered:


A.    Death Benefit                   C. EMI Support Benefit

B.    Disability Benefit              D. Comprehensive Benefit




Master Policyholder can customize the rider coverage by either choosing:

      Death Benefit or Disability Benefit or EMI Support Benefit or Comprehensive Benefit, on a standalone basis

      Or a combination of Death Benefit and Disability Benefit



Rider benefit shall be determined by multiplying the ratio of Rider Sum Assured to Base Sum Assured with the Death Benefit
under the base cover.

c)    Grace period for non-forfeiture provisions
     Grace period for Rider Policy will be same as per the
     Base Policy and is the specified period of time
     immediately following the premium due date during
     which a payment can be made to renew or continue a
     policy in force without loss of continuity benefits such
     as waiting periods and coverage of pre-existing
     diseases. If a valid claim has occurred during grace
     period, then Rider Benefit will be paid after deducting
     due premium. Grace period will not be applicable under
     Single Premium payment option.

     Ageas Federal Life Insurance shall be responsible to
     honor any claims brought under this policy in instances
     wherein the Master Policyholder has collected the Rider
     Premium but has failed to pay the same to the Insurer
     within the Grace Period due to administrative reasons.
