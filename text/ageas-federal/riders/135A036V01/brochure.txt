Rider can be attached to the base plan at inception or at any monthly policy anniversary of the base plan during the premium
payment term of the base policy as per the Rider Terms and Conditions.


This Rider will be attached only to Unit Linked, Non-Participating Individual products.


Policyholder shall choose the Rider Option, Rider Sum Assured, Rider Policy Term, Life Cover Option, and Rider Premium Payment
Term at inception of the rider cover, subject to the rider boundary conditions. The premium for the rider is level premium and will
be payable in addition to the base premium.




One out of the following two Rider Options can be selected by Policyholder:


    1       ACCIDENTAL DEATH BENEFIT

If Accidental Death Benefit is chosen, on Accidental Death of the life assured, benefit equal to Rider Sum Assured shall be
payable in lumpsum, provided the rider cover is in force.


2X Benefit:
In case of Accidental Death of the life assured in Airplane or Train Accident, benefit equal to 200% of Rider Sum Assured shall
be payable in lumpsum, provided the rider cover is in force.


Upon payment of death benefit under the rider, the rider cover shall terminate.
