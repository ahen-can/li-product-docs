           Assured’s death.

             fide fare paying passenger of a recognized airline flying on regular routes and on a scheduled timetable.

             Accidental Death due to ‘Train Accident’ will be payable only if the policyholder is travelling as a bonafide
             fare-paying passenger on a reserved ticket in any passenger carrying train of Indian Railways. The passenger’s
             name must be there in the Reserved Ticket List/Chart prepared by Indian Railways. Train Accident, herein,
             means an “accident’ of the nature described in Section 124 of Indian Railways Act,1989.

             In case where any of the above covered events happen beyond Rider Policy Term but within 180 days of
             occurrence of accident, claim shall be payable, provided the accident occurred during the Rider Policy Term.
