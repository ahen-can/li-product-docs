                                                                                           Introduction

                                                                        Life is full of possibilities—and while we hope
                                                                        everything goes smoothly, it’s always wise to be
                                                                        prepared. That’s where the Waiver of Premium
                                                                        benefit comes in.

                                                                        With the Ageas Federal Life Insurance Waiver
                                                                        of Premium Rider, your insurance plan stays
                                                                        secure, no matter what. In case of certain health
                                                                        conditions or life events, this rider ensures that
                                                                        your future premiums are taken care of. Your
                                                                        policy continues, your goals stay on track, and
                                                                        your loved ones remain protected —without
                                                                        any extra financial stress.




                                                                        ‘Ageas Federal Life Insurance Waiver of Premium Rider ’will be
                                                                        hereafter referred to as ‘WOP Rider’ throughout the document.

premium for the rider is level premium and will be payable in addition to the base premium. Rider Policy Term
and Rider Premium Payment Term shall be aligned with the outstanding base policy premium payment term.



The policyholder may select one or more of the following rider options at the time
of rider inception:


  1     WAIVER OF PREMIUM ON DEATH

In case of the death of the Life Assured under the WOP rider while both the Base Policy and this Rider are in force,
all future premiums of the Base Policy (including modal premiums and underwriting extra premiums) and any
other rider premiums, if applicable will be waived by the Company.

Upon waiver of all future premiums, the rider policy shall terminate and all rights, benefits and interests under
the rider policy will stand extinguished.




  2     WAIVER OF PREMIUM ON CRITICAL ILLNESS

If Life Assured under the WOP rider is diagnosed with any of the covered critical illnesses, and both the
Base Policy and this Rider are in force, all future premiums of the Base Policy (including modal premiums and
underwriting extra premiums)and any other rider premiums, if applicable will be waived by the Company.
The above benefit is applicable only if:

• The diagnosis is confirmed after the waiting period, and
• The Life Assured survives the required survival period
