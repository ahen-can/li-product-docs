                                                                    Introduction
                                             Life can be uncertain, and a critical illness can strike without
                                             warning which can prove to be devastating. It not only impacts
                                             the patient’s physical health but also takes a toll on the
                                             family’s financial health. With a Critical Illness Rider you can
                                             ensure that the path to bouncing back is not hampered by
                                             financial constraints.

                                             Introducing Ageas Federal Life Insurance Critical Shield Rider,
                                             a rider that covers up to 50 critical illnesses and ensures a
                                             comprehensive financial coverage to ensure complete focus on
                                             the path to full fitness. This rider, when attached to your base
                                             life insurance policy, provides a lump sum payment if you're
                                             diagnosed with a covered critical illness like cancer, heart
                                             attack, stroke, or kidney failure, among others. The payout can
                                             help cover medical bills, treatment costs, or everyday
                                             expenses, giving you the financial support you need to focus
                                             on your recovery. Ensure peace of mind for yourself and your
                                             loved ones—because your health should be your top priority.
                                             ‘Ageas Federal Life Insurance Linked Critical Shield Rider’ will be hereafter
                                             referred to as ‘Linked Critical Shield Rider’ throughout the document.

The Rider options provide an assured benefit to the life assured on being diagnosed with any one of the specified
Critical Illnesses, as per the chosen variant during the term of the rider. The benefit under this rider is payable in
addition to the base plan benefit(s).

A waiting period of 90 days from the risk commencement date of rider or the date of revival of rider, whichever is
later, is applicable for admissibility of a rider claim. No waiting period is applicable for Critical Illness claim arising
solely due to an accident.

An accident means sudden, unforeseen and involuntary event caused by external, visible and violent means.

A survival period of 30 days is applicable.

Survival Period refers to the period from the date of diagnosis and fulfilment of the definition of the conditions
covered for which the life assured must survive. The rider benefit shall be payable only on diagnosis of the claimed
conditions covered while the life assured is alive (claim would not be admitted if the diagnosis is made postmortem).
