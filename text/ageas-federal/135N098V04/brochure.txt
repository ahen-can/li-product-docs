                                  Introduction

In an ever-changing world, ensuring the financial well-being of your loved ones is of paramount
importance. Introducing Ageas Federal Life Insurance MAGIC Savings Plan (hereinafter referred to
as MAGIC Savings Plan), A Non-linked, Non-Participating, Individual, Savings, Life Insurance Plan
designed to provide you and your loved ones with a secure and prosperous future, irrespective of
life's uncertainties. This unique plan offers five variants, each tailored to meet your specific financial
goals and requirements, with all benefits guaranteed.


One of the most compelling aspects of this plan is that all the benefits it offers are guaranteed. You
can be rest assured that your financial future is safeguarded, regardless of market fluctuations or
economic uncertainties. This element of certainty allows you to grow your capital confidently,
knowing that you and your family are protected.


Embrace the MAGIC Savings Plan and take a step towards a brighter and more secure financial
future. With its diverse range of variants and guaranteed benefits, this life insurance policy allows
you to plan for your dreams and safeguard your loved ones' futures simultaneously. Grow your
capital with certainty and embark on a journey of financial prosperity with MAGIC Savings Plan.

                                                     Income Period


On the Maturity Date or any time during the Income Period, the policyholder has an option to receive a Present Value
of outstanding Maturity Benefit at Prevailing Interest Rate, as a lumpsum. Any accrued Maturity Benefit, if not already
paid, shall also become payable.

Where Prevailing Interest Rate is defined as:

Prevailing Interest Rate shall not be more than the Prevailing Yield on 10 Year G-Sec with a spread of not exceeding
50 basis points.

Prevailing Yield on 10 Year G-Sec is determined as an average of weekly annualized 10-Year G-Sec yields sourced
through FBIL, for Quarter 1 of that financial year, rounded to 3 decimal places.

As on 1st July’26, the Prevailing Yield on 10 Year G-Sec is 7.1% p.a. (calculated as average of weekly annualized 10
Year G-Sec yields for Quarter 1 of FY 2026-27 [i.e. from 1st April’26 to 30th June’26]). The Prevailing Interest Rate is
7.6% p.a.

The interest rate used for the calculation of Present Value is not guaranteed and shall be revised by Company,
subject to necessary approvals as required by IRDAI.

On payment of last instalment of Maturity Benefit, the policy shall terminate and all rights, benefits and interests
under the policy will stand extinguished.
