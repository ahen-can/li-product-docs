# Source lists used to build master_list.csv

| Folder | What | Publisher | As of | How obtained |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `life-council/` | "Life Insurance Company wise In-Force Products, Sep-2026 (Provisional)": product name, individual/group, insurer-reported category, UIN | Life Insurance Council | Sep 2026 | Report generated by the user at https://www.lifeinscouncil.org/industry%20information/ProductsOfLifeInsurers |
| `irdai-list-of-life-products/` | One Excel file per insurer: every product and rider UIN version, open/close dates, withdrawn status | IRDAI | files uploaded 30–31 Dec 2025 | Downloaded from https://irdai.gov.in/list-of-life-products; the per-file URL is in `SOURCES.csv` |

Known gaps:
- **Council:** no individual products shown for SBI Life, Bajaj Life, Edelweiss Life or Canara HSBC; still lists Exide Life (merged into HDFC Life).
- **IRDAI page:** no files for LIC, SBI Life, Axis Max Life, Tata AIA, Pramerica or Go Digit; uses some old company names.

## master_list.csv: build status
**v2 (3 Nov 2026):** website-first (`sources/websites/`, `scripts/website/`). The insurer website is the source of truth; the Council list is the cross-check. The IRDAI list is kept here for the record only and is **not used**: it is stale, e.g. all of Bharti's IRDAI-only products turned out to be withdrawn.

- **Categories:** never inferred from names.
- **Not yet in v2:** par/non-par and the market category. These come from each brochure's IRDAI description line in the next pass.
- **Blocked sites:** ICICI, Kotak, Star Union Dai-ichi and Go Digit carry Council data only, until the user supplies their product lists.
