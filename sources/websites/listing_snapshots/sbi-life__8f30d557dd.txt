Frequently Asked Questions
What is Life Insurance?
Life insurance is a financial plan that delivers guaranteed benefits to your loved ones during the policy term. It helps your family stay supported and financially secure, making sure they are always provided for.
Why should I buy Life Insurance?
We believe life insurance acts as a safety net against uncertainties. It supports planning for your family’s well-being, handling responsibilities, and preparing for milestones like education, housing, or retirement, while moving forward with assurance.
What are the different types of Life Insurance plans?
We offer a varied range of life insurance plans designed for every goal:
Term Life Insurance: Offers high life cover for a fixed term
Whole Life Insurance: Lifetime coverage with cash-value benefits
Unit Linked Insurance Plans
(ULIPs): Offer market-linked returns
Money Back Plans: Provide periodic payouts during the policy term
Annuity Plans: Provide regular income to safeguard the sunset years
Group Life Insurance: Offered by employers for employees well being
How do I choose the right Life Insurance plan?
Start by thinking about your goals and the people who look to you for support. We guide you toward a life insurance plan that fits with your income, lifestyle, and responsibilities, so the future feels secure for your loved ones.
What is the ideal coverage amount (Sum Assured)?
Every life journey is unique, but a helpful approach is to consider life coverage of around 10-15 times your annual income. This supports your family comfort and helps keep your future plans secure.
What is the difference between Term Insurance and Whole Life Insurance?
A term plan provides life coverage for a defined period, while a whole life plan provides lifetime coverage along with additional savings benefits. Both plans meet different needs, with one supporting focused financial planning and the other offering long-term continuity.
What happens if I stop paying premiums?
We understand that life can become challenging at times. Most plans include a grace period that allows time to complete your payment and keep your cover active. If your insurance policy lapses, we guide you through simple steps to restore it.
Can I have more than one Life Insurance policy?
Yes, you can. Many people combine different life insurance plans . For example, a term plan
for high coverage and a savings plan
for long-term goals , to build strong financial stability.
What are riders or add-ons in Life Insurance?
Riders are the optional benefits that you can choose to add to your life insurance plan . They enhance your coverage with added features such as:
Accidental Death Benefit
Critical Illness Cover
Waiver of Premium
Disability Benefit
Are the premiums eligible for tax benefits?
You are eligible for Income tax benefits/ exemptions as per the applicable income tax laws in India which are subject to change from time to time. Please consult your tax advisor for details.
How can I file a claim?
We have kept the claim process simple and caring. Inform us at the earliest, submit the claim form and required documents, and we guide your family through every step with care.
What happens if the policyholder dies after the policy term ends?
The benefits is to be received by the nominee and will depend on the terms and conditions of the product selected by the policyholder.
Can I change my nominee later?
Yes, nominee details can be updated at any time during the insurance policy term . Keeping information updated helps ensure your loved ones receive benefits as planned.
Is a medical test mandatory to buy Life Insurance?
Not necessarily. Depending on the life insurance plan and coverage amount, a medical test may or may not be required. This requirement is determined as per the board-approved underwriting policy.
