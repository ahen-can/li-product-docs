What is Term Insurance?
Term insurance is a type of life insurance that provides pure financial protection to your family for a specific period of time, also known as policy term. If the insured passes away during this period, the nominee receives full sum assured as a tax-free payout, helping the family cover expenses, maintain standard of living in their absence and repay loans if any. If the policyholder survives the term, no maturity benefit is typically paid (except in special return-of-premium plans). Term insurance offers high coverage at relatively low premiums, making it an affordable way to protect against life’s uncertainties.
How Term Insurance Works:
Step 1:
Application
Rahul, a 20-year-old non-smoker, wants to financially protect his family. He applies for a ₹1 crore term insurance plan by filling in his personal details and submitting the required documents.
Step 2: Customization
Rahul chooses the most suitable sum assured, policy term, and coverage options based on his financial needs and eligibility.
Step 3:
Evaluation
Once the application is submitted, review happens on Rahul’s details like his lifestyle, health status, and the documents provided. This process helps the insurer assess the risk and verify the information.
Step 4:
Policy Issuance
After successful evaluation, Rahul’s application is approved. Since he is a non-smoker and meets the required criteria, his premium is fixed at ₹512* per month, and the policy is issued.
Step 5:
Protection Phase
Rahul stays covered throughout the chosen policy term, as long as he continues to pay the premium on time. If he unfortunately passes away during the policy term, his family receives the ₹1 crore sum assured, which is generally tax-free as per prevailing tax laws.
Step 6:
Policy End
If Rahul outlives the policy term, the coverage ends and no payout is made under a basic term insurance plan. However, if Rahul passes away during the policy term, his nominee receives the ₹1 crore death benefit.
Let’s understand how term insurance works with a simple example.
Rohit buys a term insurance plan and pays his premium regularly. As long as he continues paying on time, his policy remains active and his life stays covered for the entire policy term.
Scenario 1: In Case of Unfortunate Event
If Rohit passes away during the policy term, his nominee (family member) receives the full sum assured as a lump-sum payout, after claim approval.
This amount is generally tax-free as per prevailing tax laws and helps the family manage expenses, loans, and future needs.
Scenario 2: If Rohit Survives the Policy Term
If Rohit completes the policy term safely, no payout is made under a standard term insurance plan, and the policy simply ends.
However, if Rohit has chosen a Return of Premium (ROP) term plan, he may receive the total premiums paid back at maturity, depending on the policy terms and conditions.

How does a Term Insurance Plan Work?
Before we talk about how it works, you should know that a term insurance plan is a legal contract or agreement between the policyholder and the insurance company. The owner (policyholder) agrees to pay for a desired term and the insurance company agrees to pay a specific death benefit to the beneficiary or beneficiaries listed in the policy. The benefits are tax-free if they meet the certain conditions. Now, let’s take a look at step-by-step breakdown of how a term insurance plan works.
Application process: The application process is the mandate which will decide further if you will be assigned a term insurance policy or not. The process will require you to provide details related to your age, occupation, lifestyle and other things. You will also have to submit the necessary documents and proofs.
Customise as Per Your Needs: Choose the policy term, death benefits, features, riders (additional benefits) and coverage amount as per your needs. You should look for a term insurance plan that offers you flexibility so that you can modify it as per your growing needs, when you get married or have children.
Underwriting and Evaluation: Once you have submitted the application, the insurer will conduct a background check. This process of evaluation is also known as underwriting. After the evaluation process, you will receive the final details. Make sure you inform your nominee too and pay the premiums timely.
Features of Term Life Insurance
Let’s take a detailed look at the features of term insurance plans.
Affordable & Accessible : Being the most cost-effective type of life insurance, premiums are lower and the cash value
doesn’t accumulate in term insurance plans. This even makes it accessible to a wider range of individuals.
Flexibility : Policyholders can choose the desired ‘policy term’ based on their needs and financial goals. The flexibility also extends to the way you pay (be it monthly, yearly, or even as a lump-sum one-time payout) and the way the beneficiary will receive the payment. For instance, the policyholder can choose from lump-sum payouts or staggered payouts, as per the family needs.
Death Benefit : Term insurance plans provide death benefit (or sum assured) to the designated beneficiaries in case of policyholder’s death during the term. There may be no maturity benefits included in term plans. If the policyholder survives, they do not receive any payout from the insurance company. There may be exception in TROP ( Term Plan with return of premium
) where the policyholder will receive a lump-sum of all the paid premiums if he/she survives the term.
Riders for Added Protection : Term plans even offer the option to get additional add-ons like critical illness cover, accidental death benefit, or waiver of premium enhance your policy.
Tax Benefits : In addition to the above, one can even enjoy certain tax benefits. For instance, premiums paid are tax-deductible under section 80C. The death benefit is also tax-free for beneficiaries under Section 10(10D).
The best term life insurance in India will be the one that fits your needs. Compare the features, riders
, and specifications to have a better understanding.

What are the payout options available with term life insurance?
Term life insurance plans offer multiple payout options to ensure financial security for your family:
1. Lump Sum Payout
The entire sum assured is paid at once to the nominee. Ideal for clearing loans, debts, and managing immediate financial needs.
2. Monthly Income Payout
Provides a fixed monthly income for a chosen period. Helps maintain regular household expenses and long-term financial stability.
3. Lump Sum + Monthly Income
A combination option where part of the amount is paid upfront, and the rest is given as monthly income. Balances immediate and future financial needs.
4. Increasing Income Payout
Monthly income increases annually to beat inflation, ensuring better financial support over time.
Choosing the right payout option depends on your family’s expenses, liabilities, and future goals.

What should be the ideal duration of a term insurance?
Consider the below aspects to select the right duration for your term life insurance plan.
1. Cover Your Financial Responsibilities: Choose the best term insurance plan:
that lasts until your major financial obligations are covered like your child’s education, marriage, home loan or any other long-term debt.
2. Until Your Children Are Independent: If you have young kids, your term should last until they become financially independent, often around age 22–25. For example, if your youngest child is 3, a 20 or 25-year term insurance plan would be appropriate.
3. Until Retirement Age: Many people choose a term that lasts until they retire or expect to have built enough savings and assets. If you are in your 30s or 40s, you can choose term plan lasting up to 60 or 65 years.
4. If You’re Young and Just Starting Out: Locking in a 20 or 30-year term while you’re young and healthy can help you get lower premiums.
While these are general guidelines, the ideal duration for a term life insurance policy depends on your personal and financial circumstances. Find a balance between your future goals and present conditions to choose the right policy term.
Factors That Impact Term Insurance Premiums
Your term insurance premium may depend on the following factors:
Age : Younger individuals are typically charged lower premiums because they’re considered lower risk. As age increases, so does the likelihood of health issues, which may lead to higher premiums.
Health and Medical History : If you are diagnosed with any critical condition like diabetes, heart disease, or high blood pressure, you may have to pay higher premiums.
Lifestyle Choices : Every lifestyle choice that you make creates a difference. Smoking, alcohol consumption, and other high-risk habits can increase your premium significantly.
Gender : Statistically, women tend to have longer life expectancy which is another key factor in deciding life insurance premiums.
Occupation : If you are involved in jobs that involve physical risk such as mining, construction, or firefighting, you may have to pay higher premiums for your term insurance plan.
Policy Term & Sum Assured : Longer policy durations and higher coverage amounts generally mean higher premiums.
Riders and Add-ons : If you’re opting for additional benefits within your term insurance plan, your premiums will increase.
When purchasing a life insurance, make sure to maintain transparency throughout the situation. Do not hide any medical condition or habit that you’re involved in, as it can even lead to denials later.

How to claim term insurance?
Here is a step by step guide to how to claim term insurance:
Inform the Insurance Company : Notify the company from where the policy is bought from about the policyholder’s demise at the earliest through their helpline, website, or branch.
Collect Required Documents : Gather all necessary documents such as the claim form, original policy document, death certificate, nominee’s ID and address proof, and bank account details.
Submit the Claim Request : Fill out the claim form accurately and submit it along with the required documents.
Provide Additional Documents (if needed) : In case of accidental or early death, you may be asked to submit medical records, FIR, or post-mortem reports.
Verification Process : The insurance company reviews and verifies all submitted details and documents.
Receive the Claim Amount : Once the claim is approved, the amount is transferred to the nominee’s bank account.
Track the Claim Status : Keep checking the status and follow up, if required, to ensure timely processing.
How to Avoid Claim Rejection?
Here is a step by step guide to how to claim term insurance:
Provide Accurate Information before Policy Issuance : Share complete and truthful details about medical history, lifestyle habits, and existing conditions while purchasing the policy.
Pay Premiums on Time : Ensure timely premium payments to keep the policy active and valid.
Understand Policy Terms : Carefully read the policy document to know the coverage, exclusions, and conditions.
Keep Documents Updated : Maintain and update important documents such as ID proof, address proof, and bank details.
Inform Nominees : Make sure the nominee is aware of the policy details and claim process.
Update Changes Promptly : Inform the insurance company about any changes in contact details or personal information.
Maintain Proper Records : Keep copies of all policy-related documents and communication for smooth claim processing.

How does a term plan work?
A term plan is the most elementary form of life insurance. By purchasing a term insurance policy, you, as the ‘policyholder pay a nominal amount as ‘premium’ for a certain period or tenure. The earlier you purchase a term plan, the lesser will be the premium (per year) you have to pay. In case, you meet an unfortunate incident resulting in loss of your life, your family or ‘nominee’ is given the ‘sum assured’ or ‘cover’ - large lump sum amount to meet your financial liabilities. In case you wish to have an additional coverage beyond just life cover, you can choose riders, such as critical illness and disability.
Does term insurance come under 80c?
Like all life insurance products, term insurance also offers tax benefits. Primarily, you get tax rebates for the premiums paid under your policy. Premium paid for a term plan will offer tax benefits under sec 80C of the Income Tax Act. Any payout made to the beneficiaries is tax free.

The coverage or sum assured of term life insurance depends on various factors. These factors include:
● The number of dependents you have
● The kind of lifestyle you will want to provide to your loved ones in case of your untimely demise
● Your savings, expenses, as well as investment needs
● Term insurance premium you can afford to pay
● The amount needed for your children’s education, your family’s needs, medical and emergency expenses, as well as increasing inflation rate
However, you can use a term insurance calculator to calculate the term policy coverage as well as the premium amount you will have to pay for certain tenure.
We recommend you ensure term policy coverage 15-20 times your annual income to accumulate enough coverage. If you start early, you will have to pay low premiums for the same amount of coverage.
What is the best term plan?
The best term insurance is one that offers the best ‘value for money.’ Choosing the best term insurance in India is essential to provide financial security to your family even when you are not around.
The term insurance premium of your term plan, however, depends on three basic things - your age, term period, as well as the assured sum you choose.
Can I have two Term Insurance Policies?
Yes, you can buy as many term insurance plans as you need or want to invest in. You can buy all the term plans online either from one insurer or multiple insurers. With age, expenses, as well as responsibilities, grow. Thus, it makes sense if you are thinking of buying multiple term life insurance plans.
While buying multiple term insurance policies, you have to consider two important points:
● Disclose all your existing policies to your insurer while buying the new one.
● Read all the term insurance related documents carefully and understand all the features, benefits, inclusions as well as exclusions.
What is the age limit to buy term insurance?
The age limit to buy term insurance
varies based on the plan you choose. However, you can buy term life insurance online or offline if you are at least 18 years old.
Is term insurance a good choice?
Yes, term insurance can be an excellent choice for individuals seeking affordable yet high-coverage life insurance policies.
At what age should I buy term insurance?
The earlier you buy term insurance, the better. Premiums are significantly lower when you are young and healthy, and you can lock in a higher coverage for a longer duration. Ideally, it is recommended to purchase a plan in your 20s or early 30s.
Can I buy term insurance with a pre-existing condition?
Yes, you can buy term insurance with pre-existing conditions, but you must disclose them honestly. The policy may come with a higher premium, waiting period, or specific exclusions depending on the medical condition and risk assessment.
What happens if I miss a premium payment?
If you miss a premium payment, a grace period (usually 15–30 days) is provided to pay it without losing coverage. If not paid within this period, the policy may lapse, and benefits will no longer be applicable.
Can I increase my cover after buying?
Yes, some term insurance plans allow you to increase your cover through features like life stage benefits during events such as marriage or childbirth. If this option is not available, you may need to buy a new policy or undergo fresh underwriting. Premiums will increase for the additional coverage.
Is the death benefit taxable?
In most cases, the death benefit received by the nominee is tax-free under Section 10(10D) of the Income Tax Act, subject to prevailing tax laws and conditions.
What is the free-look period?
The free-look period is a review window, usually it is between 15 to 30 days after purchasing a term insurance policy during which you can cancel the policy if you are not satisfied.
Does term insurance cover death outside India?
Yes, most term insurance policies do cover death occurring outside India, provided the policy terms and conditions are met.
What documents are needed to file a claim?
Documents like claim intimation form, policy document, death certificate, identity and address proof of the nominee and nominee’s bank details.
What is the difference between term insurance and life insurance?
Term insurance is a type of life insurance that provides pure protection for a fixed period at a lower cost. It offers a death benefit only if the policyholder passes away during the term, unlike other life insurance plans that may include savings or investment components.
Can a homemaker or self-employed person buy term insurance?
Generally, a homemaker may face limitations in buying a standalone term insurance policy, as insurers primarily assess income for eligibility. However, some insurers may offer coverage if there is a financially supporting spouse, subject to underwriting guidelines. A self-employed individual can easily buy term insurance, provided they can prove a stable source of income.
What happens to my term policy after it expires?
Once your term insurance policy reaches the end of its tenure, the coverage comes to an end and no benefits are payable if the policyholder survives the term. However, if the policy includes an additional feature such as Return of Premium, the premiums paid may be refunded as per the policy terms.26. Can I cancel my term insurance policy anytime?
What is a rider in term insurance and do I really need one?
A rider is an add-on benefit that you can include in your base term insurance policy to enhance coverage for specific risks. You can opt for rider basis your requirement.
Does term insurance cover accidental death?
Yes, term insurance covers accidental death as part of the base policy, provided the policy is active and terms are met.
Can NRIs buy term insurance in India?
Yes, NRIs (Non-Resident Indians) can buy term insurance policies in India. Indian insurers allow NRIs, OCIs, and PIOs to purchase life insurance under regulatory guidelines.
What is the difference between lump sum and monthly income payout?
Lumpsum payout is one-time payment, where in monthly income payout is staggered payments over time.
Does smoking or drinking affect my term insurance premium?
Yes, smoking significantly impacts your premium. Smokers are considered higher risk, so they are usually charged higher premiums compared to non-smokers.
What is term life insurance?
Term life insurance is a pure protection plan that provides financial coverage for a specific period (term). If the policyholder passes away during this term, the nominee receives the sum assured.
How Long Should Be the Term Insurance Policy Period?
The policy term should ideally cover you until the age when your major financial responsibilities end, typically between 60 to 65 years or until retirement.
Do we get money back in term insurance?
In a standard term insurance plan, no money is paid back if you survive the policy term. It is a pure protection plan. But yes, some plans offer a Return of Premium feature, where the premiums paid are refunded if you survive the policy term.
Can I have multiple term insurance policies?
Yes, you can have multiple term insurance policies. However, it is important to disclose all your existing policies when applying for a new one. The insurer will assess your overall eligibility based on your current coverage, income, and financial profile before issuing another policy. Failure to disclose existing policies may lead to claim rejection in the future.
Is there GST on term insurance?
For individual term insurance policies, GST is now nil (0%), based on the GST Council’s September 2025 decision, effective from 22 September 2025.
