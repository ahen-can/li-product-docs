Frequently Asked Questions
What are Child Plans?
Child plans are specialized financial solutions that help parents prepare a strong future for their children. They combine savings and insurance benefits to ensure that your child’s education, marriage, and other life goals are financially secure.
What is a Child Education Plan?
A child education plan is a long-term financial solution that helps parents build a secure fund for their child’s higher education. It combines savings and insurance benefits to ensure that funds are available when needed most, whether for school, college, or specialized studies. Based on the chosen plan, it can offer guaranteed payouts or market-linked returns, ensuring your child’s aspirations are always supported with confidence.
What is the difference between a Child Plan and a Child Education Plan?
A child plan provides overall financial security for multiple life goals, while a child education plan focuses specifically on building a fund for academic expenses.
Which is the Best Child Plan in India?
The best child plan in India depends on your goals, such as education, future milestones or long-term financial support. The right plan should balance growth, flexibility, and security while aligning with your budget and priorities. Look for features such as guaranteed benefits, waiver-of-premium options, and flexible payout structures. Most importantly, choose a trusted insurer like us, known for stability, transparency, and consistent support for families.
How does a Child Saving Plan work?
A child saving plan helps you save small amounts regularly over a chosen period and provides a lump sum payout at maturity. It safeguards your savings in case of an unfortunate event.
What are the benefits of a Child Insurance Plan?
A child insurance plan provides dual benefits: financial security through life cover and systematic savings for future goals such as education or marriage. Many also include premium waiver benefits.
Which is the Best Saving Plan for a Child?
The best saving plan for a child depends on how long you plan to save and your financial objectives. Look for plans that offer guaranteed returns, partial withdrawal options, and flexibility to switch funds (for ULIPs).
When should I start investing in a Child Plan?
Start as early as possible. Planning early with child plans or child saving plans helps you accumulate a larger corpus through compounding and makes your future finances more manageable.
Are Child Plans only for education purposes?
No, child plans support more than just education needs. They can also fund other milestones such as marriage, entrepreneurship, or overseas travel.
What happens to a Child Plan if the parent passes away?
In most child insurance plans , future premium payments are waived by the insurer, and the plan continues as scheduled. The savings journey remains on track, and the child receives the maturity benefit exactly as planned.
Can I withdraw money from a Child Plan before maturity?
Yes, some child investment plans and ULIP
-based plans offer the option of partial withdrawals after the lock-in period. This allows access to funds for needs such as school fees or other important expenses.
Are Child Plans eligible for tax benefits?
Yes, You may be eligible for Income Tax benefits as per the applicable income tax laws in India, which are subject to change from time to time. You are advised to consult your tax advisor on applicable tax benefits under the policy.
How do I choose the Best Child Education Plan?
To choose the best child education plan , review your child’s age, future goals, policy term, risk tolerance, and the insurer’s track record. Comparing multiple child investment plans helps you make a well-informed decision.

What to Consider Before Choosing a Child Plan
1. Goal Clarity
Start by defining the purpose of your savings, whether it’s your child’s education, higher studies abroad, or future milestones like marriage. A clear goal helps you select the most suitable plan to support your child’s journey.
2. Security and Growth Balance
Select a child insurance plan that provides life cover along with savings. This ensures financial continuity and consistent growth to meet your child’s needs at every stage.
3. Payout Timelines
Ensure that the plan’s payout schedule aligns with your child’s key life events, such as school transitions, higher education, or career beginnings.
4. Flexibility and Affordability
Choose a plan that comfortably fits your income and allows flexibility to adjust premium amounts or tenure as your child’s needs evolve over time.
5. Trust and Experience
Partner with us, a name built on decades of trust and care, offering child-saving plans designed to nurture your child’s future with stability and confidence.
