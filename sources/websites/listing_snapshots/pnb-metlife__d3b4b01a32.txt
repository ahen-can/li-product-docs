What is a Unit Linked Insurance Plan (ULIP)
A ULIP, or Unit Linked Insurance Plan, is a financial product that combines
life insurance with investment options. When you pay a premium into a ULIP, part of it goes towards
providing life insurance coverage for you and your family, while the rest is invested in various funds
such as stocks and bonds. This means that while you are building an investment portfolio that has the
potential to grow over time, you are also ensuring that your loved ones have financial protection if
something unexpected happens.
What makes ULIPs attractive is their flexibility and dual benefit approach.
You can choose from different investment funds based on your risk tolerance and financial goals, which
makes it easier to plan for long-term needs like retirement or your child’s education. Additionally,
ULIPs often come with tax benefits under current laws, which can help lower your overall tax burden. In
simple terms, a ULIP serves as a one-stop solution for both safeguarding your family with insurance and
growing your savings through market-linked investments.

What is a ULIP?
A Unit Linked Insurance Plan (ULIP) is a financial product that combines life insurance with Market-Linked Returns. Part of the premium provides life insurance coverage, while the remainder is invested in equity, debt, or a combination of funds, allowing policyholders to potentially grow their wealth over time.
What are the tax benefits of investing in ULIPs?
Premiums paid towards ULIPs are eligible for tax deductions under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, up to ₹1.5 lakh per annum. Additionally, the maturity proceeds are tax-exempt under Section 10(10D), subject to certain conditions.
What is Net Asset Value (NAV) in ULIPs?
NAV represents the per-unit value of the investment fund in a ULIP. It is calculated by dividing the total value of the fund's assets minus liabilities by the number of outstanding units. NAV fluctuates based on market performance.
Can I switch between different funds in a ULIP?
Yes, ULIPs offer the flexibility to switch between different funds (e.g., equity, debt, balanced) based on your financial goals and market outlook. Insurers may allow a certain number of free switches per year, with charges applicable for additional switches. PNB MetLife allows unlimited number of free switches to its policyholders.
Are partial withdrawals allowed in ULIPs?
Partial withdrawals are permitted after the completion of the five-year lock-in period. There may be limits on the amount and frequency of withdrawals, and some policies may impose charges for partial withdrawals.
What is a Life Insurance Savings Plan?
A life insurance savings plan is a financial product that combines the benefits of life insurance with long-term savings. It helps you secure your family’s future while building a corpus to achieve your financial goals.
Who should consider a Life Insurance Savings Plan?
Anyone looking for a secure way to save for future financial goals, such as a child’s education, retirement, or purchasing a home, while ensuring financial protection for their family, can benefit from a savings plan.
Are there any tax benefits with a Life Insurance Savings Plan?
Yes, you can avail tax benefits under Section 80C for the premiums paid and under Section 10(10D) for the maturity or death benefits, subject to prevailing tax laws.
Is there any risk associated with Life Insurance Savings Plans?
Most savings plans offer guaranteed returns, making them low-risk options. However, returns from market-linked plans (ULIPs) may vary based on market performance.
How is this different from other investment options?
Life insurance savings plans provide a unique combination of life protection, guaranteed savings, and tax benefits, which may not be available in traditional investments like fixed deposits or mutual funds.
