Retirement Plans
When you are young and at an early stage of your career, you are always full of zeal and energy to chase dreams, fulfil goals and face the challenges life throws at you. Though money can't buy happiness, it helps us improve and maintain a lifestyle throughout. At this stage, you seldom choose not to think about the future of these aspirations- when you are at the end of your career.
Retirement planning helps you with financial freedom and taking responsibility for dependents even after stepping down from your professional role. Retirement Plans
work as savings for your second inning and an investment into a healthy and happy future. Moreover, the right retirement investment plan is a financial stressbuster in your golden age. You not only remain financially self-sufficient to look after your health or fulfil your dreams like travelling, etc., but can also take out enough funds to support your family financially!
Retirement plans in India today have become as flexible as our lifestyles. From travelling the world and setting up your business to providing for your dependents, you can invest in retirement plans as per your life goals. This has made early retirement easy for many professionals. Now, they don't have to worry about financial responsibilities and can plan a happy retirement. Aviva pension plans allow you to choose the pension instalment and tenure, providing regular income during your golden years while ensuring security for you and your loved ones.
Let's see the pension plans and how they work to make you financially independent after retirement.
What is a Pension Plan?
Pension plans are life/annuity plans designed to meet your post-retirement financial needs, from living expenses to medical needs. However, the best pension plan takes you beyond just meeting your daily expenses and medical needs. It makes you financially independent enough to fulfil your post-retirement dreams.
This is possible when you invest in the best pension plan at the right time. All pension plans in India are known to provide guaranteed maturity benefits.
Why do you need Retirement and Pension Plans
We have talked so much about retirement pension plans till now. Let's discuss what you get out of these retirement plans. Why should you invest in a pension plan ?
Increasing inflation
The inflation rate is growing with time, and we can only imagine how much it will cost to maintain our current lifestyle in the next twenty years. Thus, you need more than your current savings to fulfil your needs and wants post-retirement. Investing these savings in the best retirement plans in india will help you create a huge corpus.
Power of compounding
"Sooner the better," the saying stands true when considering investing in a retirement plan. That is because your savings take more time to grow, and you can benefit from the power of compounding. Let's understand this in detail. If you have started your retirement savings at Rs 1.5 lakhs per year at the age of 45, by the age of 60, you will have accumulated Rs 44 lakhs with an interest rate of 8%. However, if you start investing the same amount at 40, assuming the same interest rate of 8% by the age of 60, you will accumulate Rs 74 lakhs as a retirement corpus.

How does Retirement Planning work?
Retirement planning involves preparing financially for post-working years. Here’s a streamlined approach to effective retirement planning:
Assess Financial Status: Evaluate your current financial situation, including assets, liabilities, income, and expenses.
Set Retirement Goals: Define your retirement age and desired lifestyle to estimate the necessary savings.
Estimate Needs: Calculate future costs considering inflation, healthcare needs, and potential emergencies.
Invest Wisely: Diversify investments through options like stocks, bonds, and mutual funds. Consider enrolling in a pension scheme in India, such as the National Pension Scheme (NPS), to ensure a steady income post-retirement.
Review and Adjust: Regularly review your plan to adapt to changing financial situations or market conditions.
Plan for Contingencies: Incorporate insurance and emergency funds to cover unexpected costs in retirement.
This structured approach ensures a sufficient nest egg for a comfortable and secure retirement.
Retirement Planning Process: A Step-by-Step Guide
Embarking on a retirement plan is essential for a comfortable and financially secure retirement. Here’s a concise guide to the retirement planning process:
Evaluate Your Current Financial Situation: Start by assessing your current assets, liabilities, income, and expenditures. This will help establish a baseline for what you will need in retirement.
Set Retirement Goals: Define what age you aim to retire and the lifestyle you aspire to maintain. This will dictate how much you need to save.
Calculate Required Savings: Estimate the total savings required to fund your retirement, accounting for inflation and expected rate of return on investments.
Choose Retirement and Pension Plans: Select appropriate pension plans in India, like the National Pension System (NPS) or other mutual funds and annuities that suit your goals and risk tolerance.
Implement the Plan: Regularly contribute to your chosen retirement accounts and invest wisely to grow your retirement fund.
Monitor and Adjust: Regularly review your investments and savings plan
to ensure they remain aligned with your retirement goals, adjusting as necessary to meet changing circumstances and market conditions.
This structured approach will help you systematically prepare for a financially secure retirement.
Types of Retirement and Pension Plans
Just as with any other investment plan, you will find different types of retirement plans as well. To know which retirement pension plan is best suited to you, here we have discussed the types of pension plans.
Immediate annuity
With an immediate annuity
plan, your pension payments start right after you purchase the policy, ensuring instant income after retirement.
Deferred annuity
This type of pension plan needs you to pay premiums for the period of the policy tenure. You receive an accumulated sum after the policy term is over.
Joint life annuity
Choose this plan if you wish to provide financial security to your loved ones. With this retirement plan, you are entitled to a lifetime pension. And at any point in time, in case of your death, you spouse continues to receive the pensions.
In case you buy an annuity
with Return of Purchase Price
You get pensions for life as long as your survive and on your death, the Purchase Price is paid to your Nominee.

Importance of Retirement Planning in India
In India, where the traditional support system of joint families is eroding, retirement planning has become crucial. Here’s why planning for retirement is important:
Financial Independence: Retirement planning ensures that you are financially independent in your later years without relying on others.
Rising Healthcare Costs: As healthcare costs continue to skyrocket, a robust retirement plan can help manage medical expenses without compromising care.
Longer Life Expectancy: Increased life expectancy means longer retirement years, requiring more substantial savings to maintain your lifestyle.
Inflation: Inflation erodes purchasing power over time. Effective retirement planning helps mitigate the impact of inflation
on your savings.
Lack of Social Security: India lacks comprehensive social security for older people. Therefore, personal savings and retirement plans in India, like the National Pension System (NPS), play a vital role in securing one's future.
Achieving Life Goals: Retirement planning allows you to achieve post-retirement life goals, whether travelling, pursuing hobbies, or philanthropy.
Prioritizing retirement planning today can lead to a secure and comfortable tomorrow, ensuring peace of mind in your golden years.
Benefits of Buying Retirement Pension Plans
Regular income after retirement
When you have retired and have no primary source of income, your retirement planning comes to your aid. You receive the sum assured
from your pension plan in terms of regular income to fulfill your living needs, wishes, as well as medical expenses.
Tax benefits
Another popular benefit of the pension plan is tax benefits. What kind of tax benefits you can receive and how much amount can be exempted from your taxable income depends on the type of pension plan you have invested in. In the Accumulation Phase, you will pay the premium at regular intervals to the plan provider. When the Income Phase begins, you will be able to withdraw 1/3rd of the money saved, while the remaining amount will be used to purchase an annuity product, which will create a source of regular income for the rest of your life. The tax benefit of a deferred annuity is that it lets your income grow tax-free exempted during the Accumulation Phase. This means you will not have to pay any taxes on the money that accumulates during the time of premium payment.

Where should you need to invest for retirement?
Choosing the right investments is crucial for effective retirement planning. Here are several recommended options:
Public Provident Fund (PPF): A secure choice offering tax-free interest and benefits, ideal for risk-averse individuals.
National Pension System (NPS): This system allows a mix of equities and debt investments tailored for retirement and offers tax advantages.
Mutual Funds: Equity-linked savings schemes (ELSS) provide high returns and tax savings under Section 80C.
Fixed Deposits and Bonds: Offer stable and guaranteed returns, suitable for conservative investors.
Real Estate: Can generate rental income and appreciate, providing a solid asset base.
Insurance Pension Plans: These plans from various insurers ensure a steady income after retirement and tax benefits.
Diversifying across these options can help manage risks and optimize returns, setting a solid foundation for your retirement.
Factors to Consider Before Planning for Retirement
Several key factors influence the effectiveness of your retirement plans in India. Here are essential aspects to consider:
Current and Retirement Age: Your age and when you plan to retire determine how long you have to save and how aggressively you need to invest.
Income Levels: Assess your earnings to establish how much you can regularly save for retirement.
Retirement Expenses: Estimate your future living costs, healthcare needs, and any unforeseen expenses to determine your required retirement corpus.
Investment Risk Tolerance: Choose investment options that match your risk appetite, from safer pension plans to riskier equities.
Inflation Impact: Account for inflation's effect on diminishing purchasing power over time.
Healthcare Needs: Plan for increasing healthcare costs, which typically rise as you age.
Considering these factors helps tailor a retirement strategy that secures your financial comfort in the later years, aligning with your long-term goals.

Eligibility Criteria for Retirement Schemes in India
Understanding the eligibility criteria for various retirement schemes in India is crucial for planning your retirement. Here are the requirements for some popular schemes:
National Pension System (NPS): Open to Indian citizens aged 18-65, including NRIs, subject to regulatory compliance.
Public Provident Fund (PPF): Available to all Indian residents, individuals can open only one account, excluding another guardian account for a minor.
Employees’ Provident Fund (EPF): Mandatory for employees in firms with more than 20 workers, with voluntary participation for high earners.
Atal Pension Yojana (APY): Targets unorganized sector workers aged 18-40, offering a fixed pension starting at age 60.
Senior Citizens’ Savings Scheme (SCSS): This scheme is eligible for individuals aged 60 and above or early retirees who invest within one month of retirement.
Choosing the right pension plan involves matching these criteria with your retirement goals.

Retirement Planning Guides FAQs
What is a Pension/Retirement Plan?
During the early stage of your career, you may want to set aside some funds/assets to meet specific goals such as travelling, pursuing your hobbies, buying a car or house of your own, etc.
At the Mid- career stage, you start with setting money aside to plan for your life after retirement, such as for living arrangements, health funds, family responsibilities and more.
Then comes the retirement age, wherein you reap the benefits of years of successful savings and investments. You no longer pay in; instead, your years of savings start to give you benefits.
Why do you need a Retirement/Pension Policy?
Although essential, retirement planning could be quite challenging in the early stage of your career. You are young and extremely busy starting off and excelling in your career, starting a family, or settling in a new location. You don’t have the time or feel the need to think about planning for your retirement just yet.
However, time flies so fast, and you will have to think of it then. So, why not start now?
Here’s how Retirement Plans in India help in keeping the financial freedom intact:
Retirement funds can help you face any unexpected circumstance, be it health issues or wealth worries, in the future.
Even after retirement, you can financially support your dependents towards their financial needs.
You can even fulfill your dreams or life goals such as travelling the world, etc.
With all of this, most importantly, you can still enjoy your financial freedom after you retire.
Not just this, a Retirement Plans helps you safeguard your dependents’ financial future; where in case of the unfortunate demise of the life insured, the nominee can avail the financial benefits of the plan.
How to choose a good Retirement/Pension Plan?
Inflation is a big factor you need to consider when you start thinking about planning your retirement. You need to take into account how much will you need in order to maintain the similar lifestyle after retirement.
For example, you currently require Rs. 1 lakh a month to maintain a good lifestyle.
20 years from now, you will have to spend Rs. 3.27 lakh a month to enjoy the same lifestyle, considering constant inflation of 6% during the period.
You may want to use a retirement calculator to know how much money would you need in future for your retirement plans in India.
While choosing a best retirement pension plans in India, think carefully about the following aspects:
Investment options available for you
Current annual earnings and estimated retirement income
Rates of return-on-investments and compound interest
Risk and return of various schemes and assets
At what age and how long from now you wish to retire
Planning for retirement is a long-term goal, and investing early will give you more time to save and invest better, in a manner that will provide you higher pay outs as retirement corpus. Your risk appetite is higher in the early stage of life, and you have more time to make up for any losses incurred over the period.
However, as you move closer to your retirement age, your risk appetite gradually scales down.

How does a Retirement/Pension Plan work?
Many investors and policy buyers often mistake a retirement investment plan as a means of savings until they are 65. Actually, if you start early and are in for long-term planning, these plans offer whopping benefits.
A Retirement Pension Plan works in two stages:
1) Accumulation stage: The stage when you start and continue to invest in policies, to build a retirement corpus.
At the start, you choose a pension plan, and the estimated amount you wish to receive upon maturity. This will help you align your policy term to your desired age of retirement.
Choice of the investment period (Premium Paying Term) – Depending on your lifestyle choices as well as investment and risk appetite, you need to decide the period for which you want to pay the premium for the plan. In case of a lump sum investment, you will be paying the whole amount at once.
2) Vesting age: The age at which your pension plan will start paying out. Your decision to quit your job at an early age will depend a lot on your financial status.
Once your retirement pension policy matures, you can avail the built corpus based on the annuity options chosen by you.
