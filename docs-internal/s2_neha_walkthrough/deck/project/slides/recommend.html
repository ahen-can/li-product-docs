Observations

Five things the comparison shows

1

Wealth Pro, Bharti’s largest ULIP, costs customers about 1.8% a year at 8% gross, against 1.0-1.1% for Growth Shield Plus and Wealth Maximizer; most of the gap is its 9% first-year allocation charge. Its exits rose from 1% of policies in FY25 to 7% in April to September 2026.

2

The market has 18 pension and 9 child ULIPs, some with an assured vesting benefit and purpose-limited withdrawals (HDFC Click 2 Retire Plus II, SBI Retire Smart Plus). Bharti has neither.

3

28 of 84 ULIPs build in waiver of premium or offer it as an option, some on death (SBI Smart Scholar Plus has it built in). Bharti’s comes only through a rider, at extra cost, on disability or critical illness.

4

Top-ups (38 of 84) and index funds (63 of 84) are common. Six Bharti ULIPs do not mention top-ups and Grow Wealth Ultra does not allow them; only Grow Wealth Ultra has index funds.

5

Better terms for online purchase (28 of 84) and for groups such as auto-debit payers, staff or existing customers (26 of 84) are widespread. At Bharti they appear in Wealth Pro, Grow Wealth and Grow Wealth Ultra.
