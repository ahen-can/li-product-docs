Where Bharti trails

The flagship is mid-pack, and whole segments are missing

Wealth Pro’s returns

Its largest ULIP costs 1.79% a year, against 1.22-1.28% at Canara and Bajaj. The 9% first-year allocation charge is only partly offset by the refunds at maturity.

No pension or child ULIP

18 pension and 9 child ULIPs on the market; Bharti has none. Pension and annuity are 2% of Bharti’s book against 18% for the industry.

Premium protection only as a rider

Waiver of premium is only in the optional Complete Shield rider, at extra cost, and only on disability or critical illness, not death (28 of 84 products have it built in or as an option; 60 in any form). No plan keeps going after death (16 of 84).

Fewer funds, no top-ups stated

8 funds in most Bharti ULIPs against 14-31 at the leaders; top-ups not stated in 6 documents and not allowed in Grow Wealth Ultra (38 of 84 allow them); additions only as a % of fund, while 32 of 84 add a fixed % of premium.

Sources: feature list v5 (deliverable 05), calculator (deliverable 06), phase 1 category workbook (deliverable 01)
