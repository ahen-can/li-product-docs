Where Bharti trails

The flagship is mid-pack, and whole segments are missing

Wealth Pro’s returns

Its largest ULIP costs 1.79% a year, against 1.22-1.28% at Canara and Bajaj. The 9% first-year allocation charge is only partly offset by the refunds at maturity.

No pension or child ULIP

18 pension and 9 child ULIPs on the market; Bharti has none. Pension and annuity are 2% of Bharti’s book against 18% for the industry.

No premium protection

No waiver of premium in any form (43 of 84 have it) and no plan that keeps going after death (16 of 84).

Fewer funds, no top-ups

8 funds in most Bharti ULIPs against 14-31 at the leaders; no top-ups (38 of 84 allow them); no guaranteed additions (20 of 84).

Sources: feature list v3 (deliverable 05), calculator (deliverable 06), phase 1 category workbook (deliverable 01)

Five Bharti ULIPs also end the policy if the fund falls below one year’s premium after lock-in, a clause only 9 of 84 products carry.
