Five moves to test with the product team

  1. Reprice or redesign Wealth Pro’s first-year allocation charge, or move volume to Growth Shield Plus, which costs customers about 0.7% a year less (on its 30-year term).
  2. Launch a pension ULIP with an assured vesting option and purpose-limited withdrawals (reference designs: HDFC Click 2 Retire Plus II, SBI Retire Smart Plus).
  3. Build waiver of premium into a return-of-charge ULIP, including on death, as an option or a child variant (SBI Smart Scholar Plus has it built in; Bharti's today is a rider that excludes death).
  4. Allow top-ups and widen the fund menu, including more index funds (63 of 84 products offer them).
  5. Consider better terms for online purchase beyond Wealth Pro and Grow Wealth (28 of 84 products) and for chosen customer groups such as auto-debit payers, staff or existing customers (26 of 84).

Ideas from the comparison, not yet costed. Pricing, persistency and channel data from Bharti would be needed before any decision

Move 1 matters most because Wealth Pro has the most policies in force of Bharti’s disclosed ULIPs. Moves 2 and 3 address the white space; 4 and 5 are smaller product changes.
