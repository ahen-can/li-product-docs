Summary

Small player, ULIP-heavy mix

Bharti is 0.3% of the Rs 1.94 lakh cr ULIP market (#16). Yet ULIP is 33% of its FY26 individual new business, and its three newer ULIPs bring Rs 212 cr of it.

Strong on charge refunds

5 of Bharti’s 7 ULIPs return mortality charges; Wealth Maximizer refunds 118% of all charges, fund management included, a design only 3 of 84 products have.

Gaps in protection and pension

Waiver of premium only through a rider (60 of 84 have it in some form, 28 built in or as an option); no top-ups stated (38 of 84); no pension or child ULIP; additions mostly a % of fund, where 32 of 84 add a fixed % of premium.

Flagship returns are mid-pack

Wealth Pro nets 6.21% at 8% gross, level with HDFC and ABSLI and behind Bajaj (6.72%) and Canara (6.78%). Bharti’s newer designs reach 6.9%.

Net yield excludes GST, as insurers illustrate; same customer for every product (male 30, Rs 1 lakh a year for 10 years, 20-year term). Features: feature list v5
