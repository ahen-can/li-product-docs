Unit-linked plans:
where Bharti stands

Bharti Life’s ULIPs against 11 peer products from SBI, HDFC, Tata AIA, Bajaj, Axis Max, ABSLI, Canara HSBC, Ageas Federal and LIC: charges, features and returns

November 2026. Sources: FY26 public disclosures, Bharti KPI disclosures, 84 ULIP policy documents read field by field

Summary

A small ULIP player with strong refunds, a mid-pack flagship and clear gaps

Small player, ULIP-heavy mix

Bharti is 0.3% of the Rs 1.94 lakh cr ULIP market (#16). Yet ULIP is 33% of its FY26 individual new business, and its three newer ULIPs bring Rs 212 cr of it.

Strong on charge refunds

5 of Bharti’s 7 ULIPs return mortality charges; Wealth Maximizer refunds 118% of all charges, a design only 3 of 84 products have.

Gaps in protection and pension

Waiver of premium only through an optional rider, not on death (60 of 84 have it in some form, 28 built in or as an option); no top-ups stated (38 of 84); no pension or child ULIP; additions mostly a % of fund, where 32 of 84 add a fixed % of premium.

Flagship returns are mid-pack

Wealth Pro nets 6.21% at 8% gross, level with HDFC and ABSLI and behind Bajaj (6.72%) and Canara (6.78%). Bharti’s newer designs reach 6.9%.

Net yield excludes GST, as insurers illustrate; same customer for every product (male 30, Rs 1 lakh a year for 10 years, 20-year term)

Where Bharti leads

Bharti’s newer designs give back more of the charges

Charge refunds

Wealth Maximizer refunds 118% of every charge, fund management included, over 5 years. Only 3 of 84 ULIPs refund fund management charges.

Low net cost

Growth Shield Plus and Wealth Maximizer cost the customer about 1.0-1.1% a year, the lowest of the 13 products we could calculate.

Free flexibility

6 of 7 Bharti ULIPs allow unlimited free switches (Future Invest: 12 a year); SBI Smart Fortune Builder allows 2 a year, HDFC Sampoorn Nivesh Plus 4. Grow Wealth Ultra adds boosters for women, ECS payers and existing customers.

Growth Shield Plus is also Bharti’s fastest-growing ULIP: new business up 48%, from Rs 53.8 cr to Rs 79.6 cr in its second year.

Sources: policy documents (features/ulip), calculator results (deliverable 06), Bharti Product Performance disclosure

Where Bharti trails

The flagship is mid-pack, and whole segments are missing

Wealth Pro’s returns

Its largest ULIP costs 1.79% a year, against 1.22-1.28% at Canara and Bajaj. The 9% first-year allocation charge is only partly offset by the refunds at maturity.

No pension or child ULIP

18 pension and 9 child ULIPs on the market; Bharti has none. Pension and annuity are 2% of Bharti’s book against 18% for the industry.

Premium protection only as a rider

Waiver of premium is only in the optional Complete Shield rider, at extra cost, and only on disability or critical illness, not death (28 of 84 products have it built in or as an option; 60 in any form). No plan keeps going after death (16 of 84).

Fewer funds, no top-ups stated

8 funds in most Bharti ULIPs against 14-31 at the leaders; top-ups not stated in 6 documents and not allowed in Grow Wealth Ultra (38 of 84 allow them); additions only as a % of fund, while 32 of 84 add a fixed % of premium.

Sources: feature list v5 (deliverable 05), calculator (deliverable 06), phase 1 category workbook (deliverable 01)

Observations

Five areas the comparison points to

1

Reprice or redesign Wealth Pro’s first-year allocation charge, or move volume to Growth Shield Plus, which costs customers about 0.7% a year less (on its 30-year term).

2

Launch a pension ULIP with an assured vesting option and purpose-limited withdrawals (reference designs: HDFC Click 2 Retire Plus II, SBI Retire Smart Plus).

3

Build waiver of premium into a return-of-charge ULIP, as an option or a child variant, as it now sits only in the optional rider (SBI Smart Scholar Plus has it built in).

4

Allow top-ups and widen the fund menu, including more index funds (63 of 84 products offer them).

5

Consider better terms for online purchase beyond Wealth Pro and Grow Wealth (28 of 84 products offer them) and for chosen customer groups such as auto-debit payers, staff or existing customers (26 of 84).

Observations from the comparison, not yet costed. Pricing, persistency and channel data from Bharti would be needed before any decision

Method

How this was built, and its limits

Method

  • Market sizes from FY26 Q4 public disclosures of 25 insurers
  • 633 products from the Life Council list and insurers’ official lists; brochures for 516
  • 84 ULIP policy documents read field by field, every value with page and quote
  • Our own ULIP calculator, within 0.3% of SBI, HDFC and Bharti illustrations

Limits

  • ICICI (12.7% of ULIP premium) not yet included; its site blocks automated access
  • Peers’ sales by product are not public, so we compare closest designs
  • Tata AIA does not publish mortality rates, so its returns are not calculated
  • Fund performance and ratings are not yet included
