# ULIP pilot: which products we compare, and why

The user's guidance: the deck shows 5 products and the supporting Excel 10 to 15. ICICI is left out for now (no documents) and Kotak is dropped. The user can change any pick in the "My view" tab of deliverable 01.

## Rule
1. **Bharti's anchor products.** Its two largest disclosed ULIPs by FY26 NBP are Wealth Pro (Rs 81.6 cr) and Growth Shield Plus (Rs 79.6 cr). Both return mortality charges at maturity, the design called "TULIP". Wealth Maximizer is added because it has the most unusual charge design (118% refund of all charges) and the best calculated return.
2. **One peer product per selected insurer**, the closest match to Bharti's anchors. Insurers don't publish sales by product, so we can't pick "best sellers". The match rule, in order:
   - a life ULIP, not pension or child;
   - regular or limited pay;
   - sold offline;
   - returns mortality charges (TULIP), or else the insurer's main wealth ULIP;
   - the latest version among equals.
3. **Two online benchmarks:** SBI eWealth Plus and HDFC Click 2 Wealth. They show what a low-charge online ULIP gives, and both are checked against the insurers' own quote tools.
4. **LIC is a reference column,** not a peer.

## Deck (5)
The deck shows Bharti Wealth Pro against the closest product of the 4 largest private ULIP sellers we have documents for: SBI (27.9% of ULIP premium), HDFC (12.5%), Tata AIA (7.2%) and Bajaj (6.2%).
